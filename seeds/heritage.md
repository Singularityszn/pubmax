# Heritage seed blurbs

Hand-written notes for "The Landlord" demo crawl. Warm and factual — no invention.
These feed `public/data/heritage_cache.json`; keep the two in sync by hand.

## Prospect of Whitby

A survivor from Tudor London, the Prospect of Whitby sits right on Wapping Wall
where the Thames still slaps against the wall below the terrace. It's usually
dated to around 1520 and is often called one of the oldest riverside pubs in the
city. If the crawl wants "old riverside London" in one stop, this is it.
— Source: seed note / Wikipedia

## The Grapes

Down on Narrow Street in Limehouse, The Grapes is a narrow, river-facing pub with
a balcony straight over the water at high tide. A tavern has stood on the site
since 1583, and the Grade II listed building has kept its long, tight riverside
room. It's also one of the pubs Alastair Hilton has photographed for his print
shop, which makes it a natural writer-trail stop.
— Source: seed note / Wikipedia

## The Dove

The Dove sits on Upper Mall in Hammersmith, tucked against the Thames with a tiny
front room that holds a Guinness World Record for the smallest bar in a public
house. Grade II* listed and much loved, it's the west London water-side anchor
for a heritage route.
— Source: seed note / Wikipedia

## The Old Pack Horse

On Chiswick High Road, The Old Pack Horse wears its Edwardian face proudly — the
current building was put up in 1910 for the Isleworth Brewery and is Grade II
listed. It's a good landmark stop when the crawl heads west out of the centre.
— Source: seed note / Wikipedia

## The Lamb

The Lamb on Lamb's Conduit Street is a classic Bloomsbury Victorian, best known
for the etched "snob screens" that still swivel above the bar — a rare survival
of the period's saloon-and-public divide. Grade II listed and quietly proud of
its preserved interior.
— Source: seed note / Wikipedia

## The Sun Tavern

A Bethnal Green pub that turns up in Alastair Hilton's print shop, The Sun Tavern
is kept here as a writer-inspired East End seed for the crawl. Treat the heritage
detail as a soft lead until it's sourced.
— Source: seed note / Alastair Hilton print shop
