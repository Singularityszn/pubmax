Method and sources

Only prices with a public source and date are published. Older map-only prices stay out.

What counts. Community submissions, a pub or brewery’s own published material, and properly licensed open data may enter the public Index only with a public source URL and price date.

Boroughs. We place coordinates inside versioned Greater London boundary shapes. A point outside every shape gets no borough; we never assign it to an arbitrary nearby one.

What stays out. The map may still use an older price baseline. Those rows stay out of this public Index, its CSV and its structured data.

Dates. Price dates come from the source record. Build time and file modification time are never presented as when a price was seen.
