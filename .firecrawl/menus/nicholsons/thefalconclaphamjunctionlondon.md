## Cask Ale, Craft Beer & Cider

We pride ourselves on serving exquisite, perfectly poured cask ales.

Brewed by independent Cornish family brewers, St Austell Brewery, our very own Nicholson's Pale Ale has been a permanent fixture in each of our pubs since it was first launched in 2012.

Fine malts provide the perfect base to this beautifully balanced ale. Drawing on over 160 years of experience, St Austell Brewery lovingly crafts and brews Nicholson's Pale Ale with expert brewing know-how at it's base in Cornwall.

So, raise a glass with us, and toast 150 years of Nicholson's with a pint of our finest pale ale.

Please ask your server or visit the bar to see the full range today.

### Nicholson Gin

Nicholson's Pubs and Nicholson Gin have a special relationship that dates back to 1873 when William Nicholson, founder of Nicholson Gin bought the freehold of six pubs that hadn't paid their gin bills. This was the beginning of the Nicholson's Pub Collection.

Over a century and a half later, the Nicholson family is still directly involved with Nicholson Gin, proudly continuing the legacy of William Nicholson.
