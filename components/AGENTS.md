# Components, map canvas and the design system

Rules whose subject is a rendered surface: the MapLibre canvas, the sheets and chrome, and the launch primitives.

Repo-wide laws and the index of every other area file are in the root [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md).

Long-form incident history, measured proof and review finding IDs live under [`docs/rules/`](../docs/rules/). The bullets below state each invariant in brief and link to the owning detail.

## Map canvas and pins

- **A camera move has an OWNER, and the map a reader is looking at loads first.** The canvas takes ONE `focusPoint` fed by TWO owners, the opening-location answer and the area lane (everything through `moveMapCameraTo`), so the token is an IDENTITY carrying its owner (`lib/mapCameraFocus.ts`), never a bare counter each owner starts at 1. A viewport that names nowhere may not be read from: while the opening question is open the map's placeholder bounds are the WHOLE WORLD, so `openingLoadViewportFor` answers the city's own view, and the ring around a settled viewport waits for idle while the viewport keeps the caller's turn (`VIEWPORT_SHARD_RING` / `NEIGHBOUR_SHARD_RING`). Captain's law: only that map loads, then the sides fill in. The `/map` ceiling in `perf/route-budgets.json` comes DOWN at the next CI sweep, never up. Pins: `__tests__/mapCameraFocus.test.ts`, `__tests__/mapViewportFirstShards.test.ts`. Detail: [`../docs/rules/components-map-canvas-and-pins.md#a-camera-move-has-an-owner-and-the-map-a-reader-is-looking-at-loads-first`](../docs/rules/components-map-canvas-and-pins.md#a-camera-move-has-an-owner-and-the-map-a-reader-is-looking-at-loads-first).
- **Map density is a contract, not a styling choice.** The pin and cluster zoom boundaries, the supercluster radius and the symbol-collision policy with its narrow overlap exemptions are the constants at the top of `components/map/canvas/buildScene.ts`, asserted in `__tests__/mapSymbolCollision.test.ts`. Change them there, and keep every app symbol layer inside MapLibre's collision index. Detail: [`../docs/rules/components-map-canvas-and-pins.md#map-density-is-a-contract-not-a-styling-choice`](../docs/rules/components-map-canvas-and-pins.md#map-density-is-a-contract-not-a-styling-choice).
- **A pin's COLOUR may be a hint; a pin's FIGURE may not.** Pins print the price itself from `PIN_PRICE_LABEL_MIN_ZOOM` (`components/map/canvas/filters.ts`, which owns the gate and the `text-field` expressions; the tag's size, offset and padding are at the top of `components/map/canvas/buildScene.ts`). The default pint band (`priceBucket`) may be fed by a demo seed so an unpriced city pack still reads as a map; its label is a claim, so `formatPinPriceLabel` in `components/map/canvas/geojson.ts` reads a strictly narrower stack (contributor price, curated `cheapestPrice`, favourite-pint price, never `latestDemoPrice` and never a `priceBand` without a price) and only for pub kinds (`isPubVenueKind`). A selected non-pint lens replaces both band and label from its corroborated category price, names the drink in the label, and makes missing prices neutral. A famous bar, late-food or restaurant venue's default `cheapestPrice` is its anchor price, which printed bare would masquerade as a pint. An uncorroborated report cannot reach either lane. The tag rides `pubs-point` itself and takes the ordinary collision deal (`text-optional`, no `*-allow-overlap`), so where it will not fit the LABEL goes and the pin stays. Pins: `__tests__/mapSymbolCollision.test.ts`, `__tests__/canvas-geojson.test.ts`. Detail: [`../docs/rules/components-map-canvas-and-pins.md#a-pin-s-colour-may-be-a-hint-a-pin-s-figure-may-not`](../docs/rules/components-map-canvas-and-pins.md#a-pin-s-colour-may-be-a-hint-a-pin-s-figure-may-not).
- **The live Pint Index arrival must full-load the map.** The area chips in `components/pintindex/PintIndexArrival.tsx` are plain anchors on purpose: the map freezes at mount whether an arrival is intentional (`lib/explicitMapIntent.ts`) and what the camera owes it, so a soft navigation hands it an empty query and opens all of London. They deep-link `?sel=<venue>`, not `?q=<area>`. The arrival funnel's events are in `lib/analyticsEvents.ts` and `docs/METRICS_FUNNEL.md`. Detail: [`../docs/rules/components-map-canvas-and-pins.md#the-live-pint-index-arrival-must-full-load-the-map`](../docs/rules/components-map-canvas-and-pins.md#the-live-pint-index-arrival-must-full-load-the-map).
- **A MAP CONTROL BESIDE THE VENUE DRAWER IS AN EXEMPT SURFACE, NEVER A HOLE IN THE TRAP.** Captain 13 Sep 2026 (lane 5): the desktop drawer's `map-surface` focus trap inerted the whole map stage section, so the mapped-route chip's `Check last train at final stop` took no click or Enter, and the chip sat under the drawer's left edge at 1440. A control that must stay live while the drawer is open carries `data-focus-trap-exempt` (`FOCUS_TRAP_EXEMPT_ATTRIBUTE`, `lib/useFocusTrap.ts`). THREE rules. (1) Inert is inherited, so the trap walks DOWN a sibling that holds an exempt surface and inerts only the children beside its path (`inertTargets`), re-derived while the trap holds, because adding a second stop from the drawer mounts the chip. (2) Tab from the drawer's last control reaches the exempt surface and returns (`nextTrapFocus`), and the drawer drops `aria-modal` while the chip is live; when an exempt surface leaves with focus inside it (the chip's `Hide`), focus returns to the drawer's first control; a `strict-modal` trap exempts nothing. (3) An exempt chip takes the map lane the drawer leaves from 769px, where the drawer is a side panel, at its full 44px buttons; from 641px to 768px the drawer is a full-width bottom sheet with no lane, so the chip leaves the layout while it is open (`components/map/mapBannerStaging.css`). Pins: `__tests__/focusTrapExemptSurface.test.ts`, `e2e/drawer-route-chip-reachable.spec.ts`. Detail: [`../docs/rules/components-sheets-chrome-and-navigation.md#a-map-control-beside-the-venue-drawer-is-an-exempt-surface-never-a-hole-in-the-t`](../docs/rules/components-sheets-chrome-and-navigation.md#a-map-control-beside-the-venue-drawer-is-an-exempt-surface-never-a-hole-in-the-t).
- **A LANDMARK STORY LIVES IN THE FRAME EACH WIDTH ALREADY OWNS, and the canvas only reports the tap.** Captain 2026-09-05, from a phone at 07:41: the story was a card `PubMapCanvas` pinned over the map, so on a phone its title sat under the chip row, pins showed through its edges and its last rows hid under the planning pill and the dock, while at 768 and 1440 it sat under the toolbar and the banners. `PubMap` owns the selection (synced to `?landmark=` as before), and ONE body, `components/map/LandmarkStoryBody.tsx`, is rendered in two frames: the phone's shared `Sheet` as kind `landmark` (the landmark's name is the chrome's one heading, so the body never repeats it) and the desktop's LEFT drawer, the planner's frame, so the toolbar and the ambient banners leave its lane the way they leave the planner's (`story-open` beside `planning-open` in `components/map/mapToolbar.css` and `components/map/mapBannerStaging.css`, and `MapToolbar` takes `storyOpen` because its lane is a SPRING that reads the open drawer's width, never CSS alone). A landmark pick leaves every other surface the way a pin tap does, and a venue pick retires the story ON SCREEN while the trail keeps it: the story is a `landmark` entry in `useMapSurfaceNavigation` (state `landmarkId`, title the landmark's name), `coordinatedMapOverlay` answers `landmark` under the venue and the planner, so a pub opened out of the story offers `Back to Covent Garden` and the browser's Back restores the story (verify-preview-4 J02), and the phone's planning pill is not painted under the sheet. FOUR rules ride with it. (1) A Wikimedia hero is asked through Special:FilePath and the browser follows its redirect chain, so EVERY host that chain lands on is in the CSP `img-src` (the thumb host for a sized file, measured 2026-09-05); a host missing there draws a broken-image glyph with the credit bar still under it. (2) A failed photo swaps the whole figure for the brand treatment and the credit leaves with it (`components/LandmarkHeroPhoto.tsx`), because a credit is a claim about the bytes on screen. (3) The story says where it is (`lib/landmarkArea.ts`, `In Piccadilly & Soho`) rather than the top bar being rewritten, because the bar prints the area the reader CHOSE; only an area whose own radius covers the point may claim the landmark. (4) A nearby distance prints through `formatLogNearbyDistance` alone and the straight-line caveat is said ONCE in the section head (`STORY_PUBS_DISTANCE_CAVEAT`), on the map and on `/landmark/[id]` alike. - **THE MAP ARRIVES WITH ONE SLIGHT TURN, AND THEN HOLDS STILL.** Captain 7 Sep 2026: "I also want the map to rotate slightly." Measured first: the shipped map arrives FLAT, bearing 0 and pitch 0, because the canvas is built while the opening-location question is still open and takes the hold view's attitude, and the focus move that follows carries centre and zoom only. `lib/mapArrivalBearing.ts` is the whole rule and a pure leaf: ease 0 to 4 degrees over one second, once per mounted map, then nothing. FOUR cases make no move at all - reduced motion, a deep link that named a pub or a landmark, a bearing somebody already owns, and a bearing that is not a number. It is NOT the idle orbit returning (`__tests__/idleOrbitRemoved.test.ts` still keeps that gone): `arrivalBearingSpentRef` spends it once, because `mapInstanceReady` is set once on first mount and never changes, preventing the arrival bearing effect from restarting across WebGL context rebuilds. It rides the one camera lane as a REACTIVE intent, so a reader holding the map keeps their own attitude (`lib/mapGestureGuard.ts`). TWO further facts, both measured on 7 Sep 2026. (1) THE TURN WAITS FOR THE MAP TO STOP, and the window lives in the rule leaf because the browser spec waits out the same window: without the wait the opening-location answer cancelled the turn's pending frame on the latest-wins camera lane, and with the spec keeping its own shorter copy it read the bearing DURING the wait and reported a flat map on a build that turns in 3.9 seconds. (2) THE MAP ARRIVES AT ONE OF TWO ATTITUDES and the app does not pick which: flat 0 when the canvas is built while the opening-location question is open, or the city's own designed bearing (London -8) when that question answers first. A flat map turns and an attitude is kept, so the map is off north either way; a browser spec may assert THAT, and only the unit test may assert the four. Detail: [`../docs/rules/components-sheets-chrome-and-navigation.md#the-map-arrives-with-one-slight-turn-and-then-holds-still`](../docs/rules/components-sheets-chrome-and-navigation.md#the-map-arrives-with-one-slight-turn-and-then-holds-still).
- **Phone chrome is measured, not eyeballed, and a trust caption never ellipses.** Phone rows share the balanced outer boundary published in `components/mobile/mobileMapShell.css`; the Tonight Arc reserves the `--mobile-map-corner-lane` inside it so the fixed TfL control and a right safe-area inset cannot steal its taps. `e2e/mobile-map-chrome-fit.spec.ts` owns the rendered 320, 390 and 430 geometry, horizontal scrolling, centre-point hit ownership and the budget guarantees; `__tests__/mobileChromeFit.test.ts` owns the shipped-CSS contract, the tap-target floors, and the rule that the venue price caption wraps rather than truncating. Do the measuring in a real device viewport: `chrome-devtools-axi open` drops the CDP device override, so `open`, then `emulate`, then reload in place, because a second `open` resets it and the mobile shell only mounts if the page booted at phone width. A truncated price caption cuts the meaning the whole trust argument rests on: widen the cell or wrap the line, never ellipsis it. The phone tab bar ALSO answers to the on-screen keyboard, which no phone browser shrinks the layout viewport for. `lib/softKeyboard.ts` is the ONE rule and it needs BOTH halves, a focused text field AND a visual viewport that has lost `SOFT_KEYBOARD_MIN_SHRINK_RATIO` of the layout one, because a caret alone is a physical keyboard and a shrink alone is the URL bar. It hides by TRANSFORM in the open-sheet idiom, never by dropping the reserved bottom clearance, or the page reflows under the caret; a focus recompute is deferred by a task, because `focusout` fires before the next field takes focus. Pins: `__tests__/softKeyboard*.test.ts`. - **THE PRODUCT ANSWERS FIRST, AND THE CONSENT CARD ARRIVES AFTER THE ANSWER, DOCKED.** Captain's standing ask over PlanAstra section 3 ("the primary asks before it gives" is wrong): the analytics consent card was the first thing a new reader met on every route at every width, before any answer, covering the first listing at 360x640 on Tonight, the rail on the landing at 320x568, the city list on Places and the Founding hundred on Social.
