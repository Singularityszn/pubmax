# Components, map canvas and the design system

Rules whose subject is a rendered surface: the MapLibre canvas, the sheets and chrome, and the launch primitives.

Repo-wide laws and the index of every other area file are in the root [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md).

## Map canvas and pins

- **A camera move has an OWNER, and the map a reader is looking at loads first.** The canvas takes ONE `focusPoint` fed by TWO owners, the opening-location answer and the area lane (everything through `moveMapCameraTo`), so the token is an IDENTITY carrying its owner (`lib/mapCameraFocus.ts`), never a bare counter each owner starts at 1. A viewport that names nowhere may not be read from: while the opening question is open the map's placeholder bounds are the WHOLE WORLD, so `openingLoadViewportFor` answers the city's own view, and the ring around a settled viewport waits for idle while the viewport keeps the caller's turn (`VIEWPORT_SHARD_RING` / `NEIGHBOUR_SHARD_RING`). Captain's law: only that map loads, then the sides fill in. The `/map` ceiling in `perf/route-budgets.json` comes DOWN at the next CI sweep, never up. Pins: `__tests__/mapCameraFocus.test.ts`, `__tests__/mapViewportFirstShards.test.ts`.

- **Nothing moves this camera but the reader, and there is ONE compass.** Captain 3 Sep 2026: the idle ambient orbit is DELETED, not switched off (fence `__tests__/idleOrbitRemoved.test.ts`), because it turned the map at 0.6 degrees a second whenever a reader stopped touching it. While a gesture is on the glass no camera intent runs at all, and for `GESTURE_CAMERA_HOLD_MS` after it only the REACTIVE intents (`route`, `query`, and the arrival turn) stay out, never a move the reader asked for: `lib/mapGestureGuard.ts` is the rule and `scheduleCamera` in `components/map/canvas/useMapCamera.ts` is the one place it is asked. MapLibre's compass is off at the source, because its reset flattened the pitch to a view no city opens on; `lib/mapCompass.ts` owns the one control, which eases BOTH axes back to the city's designed attitude. It lives in the Layers popover from 7 Sep 2026 rather than on the map edge: what that rule refuses is a compass that appears and vanishes with the map's own attitude, and a control the reader opens one button to reach is not that. `components/map/canvas/cameraProbe.ts` publishes the camera to the browser suite the way `components/map/canvas/paintedPinProbe.ts` publishes the pins. Pins: `__tests__/mapGestureGuard.test.ts`, `__tests__/mapCompass.test.ts`, `e2e/map-gestures.spec.ts`.

- **Map density is a contract, not a styling choice.** The pin and cluster zoom boundaries, the supercluster radius and the symbol-collision policy with its narrow overlap exemptions are the constants at the top of `components/map/canvas/buildScene.ts`, asserted in `__tests__/mapSymbolCollision.test.ts`. Change them there, and keep every app symbol layer inside MapLibre's collision index.

- **A god component is decomposed IN PLACE, because the map's own tests read its SOURCE.** `PubMap` was a 305-branch render function and is 27 (#1185). Roughly 23 test files read `components/PubMap.tsx` as TEXT and assert on its own expressions, so the decomposition adds NO component and leaves that text where it is: pure derivations move to `lib/pubMap.ts`, a JSX subtree becomes a NESTED function inside `PubMap` (ESLint scores complexity per function, and a nested call leaves the React element tree identical where a component would add a fibre), and a derivation whose exact call text is pinned stays a module-scope helper in the component file. The expensive rule to rediscover: React Compiler treats a value handed to a helper it cannot see through as POSSIBLY MUTATED, so wrap such a call in `useMemo` or leave the expression inline, or `preserve-manual-memoization` breaks on the memo blocks near it. Pin: `__tests__/pubMap.test.ts`.

- **A pin's COLOUR may be a hint; a pin's FIGURE may not.** Pins print the price itself from `PIN_PRICE_LABEL_MIN_ZOOM` (`components/map/canvas/filters.ts`, which owns the gate and the `text-field` expressions; the tag's size, offset and padding are at the top of `components/map/canvas/buildScene.ts`). The default pint band (`priceBucket`) may be fed by a demo seed so an unpriced city pack still reads as a map; its label is a claim, so `formatPinPriceLabel` in `components/map/canvas/geojson.ts` reads a strictly narrower stack (contributor price, curated `cheapestPrice`, favourite-pint price, never `latestDemoPrice` and never a `priceBand` without a price) and only for pub kinds (`isPubVenueKind`). A selected non-pint lens replaces both band and label from its corroborated category price, names the drink in the label, and makes missing prices neutral. A famous bar, late-food or restaurant venue's default `cheapestPrice` is its anchor price, which printed bare would masquerade as a pint. An uncorroborated report cannot reach either lane. The tag rides `pubs-point` itself and takes the ordinary collision deal (`text-optional`, no `*-allow-overlap`), so where it will not fit the LABEL goes and the pin stays. Pins: `__tests__/mapSymbolCollision.test.ts`, `__tests__/canvas-geojson.test.ts`.

- **A pin's fill says the price; its EDGE is what makes it findable, and dark mode needs two tones for that.** Dark roads are subordinate warm context in `lib/mapBasemapTaste.ts`, but night ground, water, buildings and road tiers still span distinct tones, so no single rim edges every price band everywhere. `venuePinEdgeTokens` (`components/map/canvas/tokens.ts`) therefore publishes a rim plus an opposite-luminance casing in dark and nothing in light, and `makeVenuePinDraw` in `lib/mapIcons.ts` draws the casing pass first at `GLASS_CASING_STROKE` so the rim can drop to a hairline and the band keeps its area. Do not reach for a brighter band instead: `--brick` is a muted rose because dark `--brass` is a coral, and this map's rings are already spoken for. The UK base pub takes the SAME edge, for the same reason. `__tests__/mapPinBandContrast.test.ts` reads the shipped dark tokens rather than a restated copy and holds every band to an edge over every dark basemap tone.

- **The live Pint Index arrival must full-load the map.** The area chips in `components/pintindex/PintIndexArrival.tsx` are plain anchors on purpose: the map freezes at mount whether an arrival is intentional (`lib/explicitMapIntent.ts`) and what the camera owes it, so a soft navigation hands it an empty query and opens all of London. They deep-link `?sel=<venue>`, not `?q=<area>`. The arrival funnel's events are in `lib/analyticsEvents.ts` and `docs/METRICS_FUNNEL.md`.

- **A MAP THAT NEVER MOUNTS IS THE SHELL'S PROBLEM, AND NOTHING ON /map MAY WAIT FOR IT FOR EVER.** F08/J33. `components/PubMapCanvas.tsx` diagnoses every way a MOUNTED map dies and each has its own watchdog and honest heading; none can fire when the canvas never mounts, and MapLibre lives in its own chunk behind a dynamic import on purpose. Measured with that one chunk aborted: `/map`, `/map?sel=` and `/map?log=1` all answered with `app/error.tsx`, so a blocked map library took the venue list, the selected pub's sheet and the Pint Drop composer with it. `lib/mapCanvasAvailability.ts` is the whole of the shell's policy and it answers TWO things the canvas cannot: `module` (a `MapCanvasBoundary` in `components/PubMap.tsx` caught the subtree) and `timeout` (`MAP_CANVAS_READINESS_CEILING_MS` lapsed). FOUR rules. (1) The ceiling runs from mount until the canvas publishes ready OR errored, never off the loading frame, which lifts on painted pins the shell can satisfy with no canvas at all; it sits ABOVE `PIN_READY_CEILING_MS` and `FIRST_FRAME_TIMEOUT_MS` so a live canvas always names its own failure first, and the fence reads both numbers out of that file. (2) RETRY BUILDS A FRESH LAZY: React caches a rejected one for ever, so `pubMapCanvasForAttempt` keys one per attempt and remounting the same component would replay the rejection without touching the network. (3) There is ONE venue view, `components/map/MapFallbackCard.tsx`, shared by the canvas's five failure kinds and the shell's two, and ambient banners stand down while it is up because a road-closure note describes a map the reader cannot see. (4) A CANVAS IS THE ONLY THING THAT REPORTS MAP BOUNDS, and three arrival lanes (search arrival, UK place arrival, national browse) wait for that report before reading a shard: with no canvas `/map?sel=<id>` sat at ZERO venues for the life of the page while a bare `/map` loaded 141, which took the card's rows, the list and the log intent with it, so the shell hands the loader the opening view once per attempt through that same door. Two phone geometry defects rode with it: the fallback card reserved the chrome from `--mobile-map-rail-h`, a property defined NOWHERE, so the `calc()` was invalid at computed-value time and the card's heading sat behind the top bar (it reads `--mobile-map-chrome-full-h` now, published by `components/mobile/mobileMapShell.css`), and the basemap Retry cleared the tab bar alone, so `elementFromPoint` at its own centre answered the plan-activation bar and the tap was swallowed; it reads that stack member's own published berth and height instead. Pins: `__tests__/mapCanvasAvailability.test.ts`, `__tests__/mapSurfaceChrome.test.ts`, `__tests__/mobileChromeFit.test.ts`, `e2e/map-blocked-fallback.spec.ts`; proof `docs/proof/map-blocked-fallback/`.

## Sheets, chrome and navigation

- **No surface is a dead end, and the way out is one pattern.** `lib/surfaceStack.ts` holds the presentation-free trail. On the Map, `components/map/pubmap/useMapSurfaceNavigation.ts` is the only browser-history owner: every entry carries the complete trail plus venue selection state, direct opens publish their decision synchronously before React changes drawers, and its one `popstate` listener restores the landed snapshot. An internal open may only `pushState` or `replaceState`; reopening a surface already lower in the trail writes a fresh entry with the current surface as its parent and never calls `history.go()`. `components/ui/surface-nav.tsx` is the only affordance: leading Back, trailing Home, the same box in the same two cells of one head, on the phone sheet, desktop drawers and List view. Back renders only when there is a parent; Escape is keyboard Back, fling-dismiss is gesture Back, and a tap on an open overlay's own phone top-bar control is Back too, never a bare close that leaves the surface on the trail. A phone sheet MOUNTS only where its shell owns the lane (`sheetsEnabled`, `components/mobile/MobileMapShell.tsx`), because a mounted sheet still claims Escape on `window` and still captures focus, and CSS reaches neither. The sheet also OWNS ITS OWN STYLESHEET: `components/mobile/MobileSharedSheet.tsx` imports `components/mobile/mobileMapShell.css` itself, because PubMap renders the phone sheet through a static import while the shell that used to be the stylesheet's only importer is loaded dynamically, and on a `/map?sel=` arrival at 390 the venue sheet stood unstyled (`position: static`, `z-index: auto`) for the one to four seconds before the shell chunk landed (#1490). Back must restore exact held state, never reload a default, and never push history from inside a setState updater. A panel anchored to a visible trigger is not in the trail and owes only `lib/useDismissOnEscape.ts` plus outside dismiss; ambient banners and chips owe neither. Pins: `__tests__/mapSurfaceHistory.test.ts`, `__tests__/surfaceStack.test.ts`, `__tests__/mapShellSheetOwnership.test.ts`, `e2e/map-surface-history.spec.ts`, `e2e/surface-back-and-home.spec.ts`.

- **A LANDMARK STORY LIVES IN THE FRAME EACH WIDTH ALREADY OWNS, and the canvas only reports the tap.** Captain 2026-09-05, from a phone at 07:41: the story was a card `PubMapCanvas` pinned over the map, so on a phone its title sat under the chip row, pins showed through its edges and its last rows hid under the planning pill and the dock, while at 768 and 1440 it sat under the toolbar and the banners. `PubMap` owns the selection (synced to `?landmark=` as before), and ONE body, `components/map/LandmarkStoryBody.tsx`, is rendered in two frames: the phone's shared `Sheet` as kind `landmark` (the landmark's name is the chrome's one heading, so the body never repeats it) and the desktop's LEFT drawer, the planner's frame, so the toolbar and the ambient banners leave its lane the way they leave the planner's (`story-open` beside `planning-open` in `components/map/mapToolbar.css` and `components/map/mapBannerStaging.css`, and `MapToolbar` takes `storyOpen` because its lane is a SPRING that reads the open drawer's width, never CSS alone). A landmark pick leaves every other surface the way a pin tap does, and a venue pick retires the story ON SCREEN while the trail keeps it: the story is a `landmark` entry in `useMapSurfaceNavigation` (state `landmarkId`, title the landmark's name), `coordinatedMapOverlay` answers `landmark` under the venue and the planner, so a pub opened out of the story offers `Back to Covent Garden` and the browser's Back restores the story (verify-preview-4 J02), and the phone's planning pill is not painted under the sheet. FOUR rules ride with it. (1) A Wikimedia hero is asked through Special:FilePath and the browser follows its redirect chain, so EVERY host that chain lands on is in the CSP `img-src` (the thumb host for a sized file, measured 2026-09-05); a host missing there draws a broken-image glyph with the credit bar still under it. (2) A failed photo swaps the whole figure for the brand treatment and the credit leaves with it (`components/LandmarkHeroPhoto.tsx`), because a credit is a claim about the bytes on screen. (3) The story says where it is (`lib/landmarkArea.ts`, `In Piccadilly & Soho`) rather than the top bar being rewritten, because the bar prints the area the reader CHOSE; only an area whose own radius covers the point may claim the landmark. (4) A nearby distance prints through `formatLogNearbyDistance` alone and the straight-line caveat is said ONCE in the section head (`STORY_PUBS_DISTANCE_CAVEAT`), on the map and on `/landmark/[id]` alike. Pins: `__tests__/landmarkHeroPhoto.test.tsx`, `__tests__/landmarkArea.test.ts`, `__tests__/pubMapCanvasDistanceContract.test.ts`, `__tests__/clerkProxyCsp.test.ts`, `e2e/landmark-story-sheet.spec.ts`; proof `docs/proof/story-sheet-ux/`.

- **A SHEET SAYS HOW MUCH OF ITSELF IS OFF SCREEN, ONCE, AND THE SHELL CANNOT BE SCROLLED.** UI sweep 5 Sep 2026 (#1516). At 641-768 the inline map drawer is a viewport-tall bottom sheet translated down by its snap, so 45dvh of its scrollport hangs under the screen at `half`. The venue command bar restated that translate as a dvh inset, and the map shell (class `appShell`) was `overflow: hidden`, which is still a scroll container: one `scrollIntoView` on a price row scrolled the shell 403px, the drawer's box moved, the inset did not, and the bar sat mid-viewport over "Published price". THREE rules. (1) The shell is `overflow: clip`, so nothing can scroll it. (2) `SpringDrawer` publishes the px it translates by as `--drawer-sheet-offset`, and the 768 band in `app/globals.css` derives `--sheet-hidden-below` from it ONCE (the per-snap dvh declarations beside the transforms are the fallback for the frame before the spring's first) and ends the scroll body with an `::after` spacer of that height so the last row is reachable. A spacer, never padding, because Chromium measures a sticky inset from the scroller's CONTENT edge. (3) A bottom-pinned child reads that variable for its inset, and the scroll container declares its viewing region with `scroll-padding-bottom` (the hidden part plus the bar), because `scrollIntoView` and a focus scroll aim at the scrollport and would otherwise land a row under the bar. Pins: `__tests__/tabletSheetInset.test.ts`, `e2e/venue-sheet-tablet-command-bar.spec.ts`; proof `docs/proof/venue-sheet-768/`.

- **THE MAP ARRIVES WITH ONE SLIGHT TURN, AND THEN HOLDS STILL.** Captain 7 Sep 2026: "I also want the map to rotate slightly." Measured first: the shipped map arrives FLAT, bearing 0 and pitch 0, because the canvas is built while the opening-location question is still open and takes the hold view's attitude, and the focus move that follows carries centre and zoom only. `lib/mapArrivalBearing.ts` is the whole rule and a pure leaf: ease 0 to 4 degrees over one second, once per mounted map, then nothing. FOUR cases make no move at all - reduced motion, a deep link that named a pub or a landmark, a bearing somebody already owns, and a bearing that is not a number. It is NOT the idle orbit returning (`__tests__/idleOrbitRemoved.test.ts` still keeps that gone): `arrivalBearingSpentRef` spends it once, because `mapReady` flips again across a WebGL context rebuild. It rides the one camera lane as a REACTIVE intent, so a reader holding the map keeps their own attitude (`lib/mapGestureGuard.ts`). TWO further facts, both measured on 7 Sep 2026. (1) THE TURN WAITS FOR THE MAP TO STOP, and the window lives in the rule leaf because the browser spec waits out the same window: without the wait the opening-location answer cancelled the turn's pending frame on the latest-wins camera lane, and with the spec keeping its own shorter copy it read the bearing DURING the wait and reported a flat map on a build that turns in 3.9 seconds. (2) THE MAP ARRIVES AT ONE OF TWO ATTITUDES and the app does not pick which: flat 0 when the canvas is built while the opening-location question is open, or the city's own designed bearing (London -8) when that question answers first. A flat map turns and an attitude is kept, so the map is off north either way; a browser spec may assert THAT, and only the unit test may assert the four.

A truncated price caption cuts the meaning the whole trust argument rests on: widen the cell or wrap the line, never ellipsis it. The phone tab bar ALSO answers to the on-screen keyboard, which no phone browser shrinks the layout viewport for. `lib/softKeyboard.ts` is the ONE rule and it needs BOTH halves, a focused text field AND a visual viewport that has lost `SOFT_KEYBOARD_MIN_SHRINK_RATIO` of the layout one, because a caret alone is a physical keyboard and a shrink alone is the URL bar. It hides by TRANSFORM in the open-sheet idiom, never by dropping the reserved bottom clearance, or the page reflows under the caret; a focus recompute is deferred by a task, because `focusout` fires before the next field takes focus. Pins: `__tests__/softKeyboard*.test.ts`.

## Venue, plan and message surfaces

- **Arrival is the moment of togetherness, never an admin form.** An account that already owns a handle is owed NOTHING on arrival: `components/identity/AccountOnboarding.tsx` awaits the server answer, syncs `pubmax_handle` and resolves to nothing when a handle comes back. It may never answer with an owned-identity or rename surface, because `POST /api/identity/handle/claim` stores no date of birth and `GET /api/identity/onboarding` therefore reports `complete:false` for the life of every account claimed that way. Renaming lives in profile editing alone (`components/profile/PubmaxxAccountHub.tsx`), private details in `PrivateIdentityEditor`, and a failed status load never falls through to the claim form. The claim step is TWO BEATS and one action, never a required section plus an optional one. A returning account gets one warm line instead (`lib/arrivalWelcome.ts`, rendered by `components/auth/ArrivalWelcome.tsx` as a polite live region, never a dialog). `/login` is TWO DOORS sharing one link machinery: `?mode=signin|signup` differ in copy, email intent and landing, and `?from=` returns a sign-in where the nav sent it from (`arrivalDestination` refuses off-site paths and refuses to land anyone back on a sign-in page). Pins: `__tests__/arrivalWelcome.test.ts`, `__tests__/accountOnboarding.test.ts`, `__tests__/loginPage.test.ts`, `e2e/arrival-journey.spec.ts`.

- **THE OVERVIEW HAS ONE PRICE DOOR PER TRUST STATE, AND THE COMPOSER IS FOLDED BEHIND IT.** Captain's rule (core-loop battle test L03, 5 Sept 2026): one button system, one clear primary per screen. A pub Overview offered four to eight price actions on one sheet, because every block that touched a price grew its own invitation. `overviewPriceDoor` in `lib/pintTrust.ts` is the whole policy, a closed table over the five trust states: `logged-once` and `aged-out` get the confirm door from #1492 (`Still £4.50?`, seeding the Pint Drop composer), every other state and every non-drop lane gets the one log door (`LOG_PRICE_DOOR_LABEL`), and an anchor lane gets none. FOUR rules. (1) The door is decided ONCE in `VenuePriceSummary` (`components/map/inspector/VenueOverviewTab.tsx`) and appended to whichever lane block renders; no lane, nudge or block may carry a door of its own, and `FirstDropNudge` takes the door as children. (2) The community price composer (`VenuePriceEntryPanel`, `open` prop) mounts its form only when `overviewComposerOpen` says so: the sheet answered the door with a focus request, with the sign-in gate, or a ranked mission arrived for this pub (the mission IS the ask). The door folds away while the form is open, so the two never stand on one screen; the panel's effects still run folded, so the venue read and `price_submit_viewed` are unchanged. The door is the `priceDoor` class (`app/globals.css`), the same flat action accent every launch primary wears, never a price band. `UnverifiedPubSheet` keeps `VenueSheetPriceEntry` open by default and is untouched. Pins: `__tests__/overviewPriceDoor.test.ts` (policy per state, one rendered door, the fold, a source sweep for the retired doors), `e2e/overview-price-door.spec.ts` (390, signed in, five states); proof `docs/proof/overview-price-door/`.

- **THE BILL IS ASKED FOR IN FRONT OF THE PRICE, NEVER INSIDE THE DISCLOSURE.** Captain 7 Sept 2026: a new price comes with a photo of the bill. `lib/pintDropReceipt.ts` is the whole rule (when a bill is owed, what a photo may be, and every word said about it), and both price doors read it: the one-tap `VenuePriceSubmit` and the full Pint Drop composer's `SpillReceiptStep` (`components/map/composer/`). THREE rules ride with it. (1) A REQUIRED CONTROL MAY NOT LIVE BEHIND A DISCLOSURE. `Log it` is disabled without a bill, so asking for one inside "Add a photo or story" would refuse a drinker at the button for a control they were never shown. `SpillReceiptStep` renders under the price step, outside `spillExtras`, and only while the composer holds a price; a note or a photo of the pub claims nothing a reader has to check and is asked for nothing. (2) THE BILL FIRST, THEN THE PINT FOR FUN. `PINT_PHOTO_FUN_LINE` and its picker appear only once the bill is in, and neither blocks a submit. - **NEXT/IMAGE DROPS A `srcSet` PROP, AND A PROXIED PHOTO IS RESIZED THROUGH ITS LOADER.** `/pubs` shipped five photographs at their natural 1632x636 and 680x453 into a 344x168 box, 934 KB of it measured on a phone, and its load was the slowest on the site (Astra's live walk, 7 Sep 2026, finding B8). The bytes were never about the page: `/api/image-proxy` took no width and the cards emitted no `srcset`. FOUR rules. (1) THE SEAM IS THE `loader` PROP: next/image sets `srcSet` from its own attributes AFTER spreading the rest, so one passed in is silently dropped, and the first measured build shipped the natural bytes with the prop nowhere in the markup. (2) `VENUE_IMAGE_WIDTHS` (`lib/venueImages.ts`) is a CLOSED set and it is next/image's OWN candidate widths, so a srcset descriptor and the width the proxy really answers name the same number; a set of our own invention made the desktop card ask for 384 and be handed 688. The set is closed because the URL is the cache key, so an open range is a cache-buster and a CPU amplification vector on a route that fetches a remote image per request. (3) A SURFACE CAPS ITS OWN DENSITY through `maxWidth`, because a browser takes the LARGEST candidate when none is big enough: /pubs caps at 640 for a picture that sits under a gradient wash and a glyph shelf at 0.88 opacity, and the cost of that cap is named in the proof rather than hidden. (4) A COMMUNITY PHOTO IS NEVER RE-ENCODED: it is a signed Storage URL and keeps the `unoptimized` path exactly as it was, so only a `/api/image-proxy` URL is resizable. The proxy never enlarges, and a failed resize serves the original bytes, that failure being about us rather than about the picture.
