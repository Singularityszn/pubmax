# ADR 0018: Station and editorial discovery proposals

## Status

Proposed on 28 October 2026. Publication remains held pending the owner's decision.
This ADR approves no route, source fetch, merge, deployment, or migration.

## Context

The approved comparison work connects exact drink orders, walking comparisons, and group planning.
Visitors may also need a useful starting point near Liverpool Street or Waterloo.
Existing borough, drink, heritage, ledger, and crawl pages already answer related questions.
Standalone `/area/{area}` pages remain held under the governed priced-landing contract.

## Proposal

Prepare one candidate document for Liverpool Street and one for Waterloo after the comparison flow passes its checks.
Each candidate must identify the station and the entrance used for any claimed walk.
It must name exact pub branches and retain each price's drink, serving, source, and date meaning.

Use the existing station-walk evidence only where its coverage supports the claim.
A straight-line distance is not a routed walk. Missing access, opening, and event information stays unknown.
Each candidate must lead to an existing comparison, map, or planning action.

Prepare a small reviewed editorial-route proposal using existing crawl records.
It must show the actual stops, separate walking time from outing duration, and explain supported alternatives.
Public editorial routes remain separate from private generated plans and user-created crawl stories.

Before approving a new URL family, the owner must review its distinct purpose, geographic coverage, and source evidence.
The proposal must identify the canonical document and how existing pages will link to it.
Do not evade the held area policy with a renamed neighbourhood URL.
Do not create keyword combinations, public guest ballots, or indexed personal plans.

## Consequences

- Existing approved pages and the comparison tools can proceed independently.
- No new station or editorial-route entry belongs in the sitemap while this ADR remains proposed.
- Actual route usefulness and source coverage decide whether a family merits publication.
- The normal privacy, source, mobile, performance, and review requirements still apply after approval.
