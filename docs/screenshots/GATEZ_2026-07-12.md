## Regressions / defects found

- **R1 — Two "Pint" tabs in the London venue inspector (real collision).**
  The pints tab uses `shortLabel: "Pint"` (`lib/venueInspectorTabs.ts:14`) while
  London's getting-home tab derives its label from `lastRideLabel: "Last Pint"`
  (`lib/cities.ts:95`), which `lastRideTabLabel()` strips to `"Pint"`
  (`lib/lastRide.ts:69-75`). Result: the strip reads `Pub · Pint · Menu · Lore ·
  Ask · Pint` — the exact D8 "venue tab 'Pint'" defect, still open. Fix: rename
  the drops shortLabel (e.g. "Drops") or the London ride short-label so the two
  never coincide.
- **R2 — Tonight lane occluded at map open.** The "Start with a story" first-run
  sheet paints over the W1 lane; the flagship surface of the whole cycle is
  invisible until the user dismisses an unrelated onboarding sheet. Not
  diagnosed to a single line — it's a z-order/first-run-sequencing decision in
  the map shell (story picker vs. Tonight lane priority).
- **R3 — Light basemap tiles frequently un-loaded** (grey field behind the map
  sheet) at 390 in the fresh sweep. Dark paints reliably. Consistent with the
  D2 "gate pin paint on tile paint" intent not fully covering the light theme's
  slow-tile case.
- **R4 — Empty "No photo yet" header dominates photo-less venue sheets** on both
  mobile and desktop (E3′ tradeoff): the gradient fallback is full-height and
  pushes the actual Golden Thread content below the fold on first open.
