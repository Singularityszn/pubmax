# Still true? Hand-run kit

The Thursday night, run by hand. One willing pub. Same weeknight. Four weeks is the series. The bar is two nights.

This file sits with the other growth docs. [`LAUNCH_KIT_2026-11-17.md`](LAUNCH_KIT_2026-11-17.md) is the Product Hunt record for 17 November 2026. That kit is a different job. No hand-run folder existed, so the whole ritual is this one file.

These nights have not been run. This file cannot run them. The captain runs them. Every blank stays blank until the captain writes what happened in the room. This file names no pub. The captain asks the pub. If the pub has not agreed, do not name it, do not send the message, and do not go.

The £5.40 in the plan is a placeholder. Do not send it.

## 1. Thursday WhatsApp

Send this the morning of the night, after you have read the live listing. Fill every blank from that read and from replies you actually have. The listing is the guess. It is not tonight's price. A source read does not confirm what the pub charges tonight.

Write the pub name only if that pub has agreed. If none has, do not send this.

`[How many said yes]` is the number of people who replied "I'll be there". If none have, replace that whole line with "Nobody has said yes yet." Do not write a zero. Do not guess a crowd.

The time is 8pm. If the pub asked for a different time, write the time they asked for.

Do not write "Save me a seat." Do not write "confirmed." The reply word is "I'll be there."

Copy from here:

```
Still true? [Pub name], [date], 8pm.

[Drink] listed [price]. [Source], read [read date].
That listing might be wrong. Tonight we find out.

[How many said yes] said yes.
Not started. Nobody has paid yet.

Reply "I'll be there" if you're coming.
Buy your own.

Come alone. If you arrive with a mate, you split for the first pint.

Two roles, said out loud. One of you pays. The other reads the price back.

You may not order what the person on your left ordered.

The night starts when the first real price is logged.
```

Until the first real price is logged, the message stays on "Not started. Nobody has paid yet." Do not change that line because the clock hit 8.

## 3. Two nights

The captain runs these nights. This file cannot run them. They have not been run. The blanks stay blank.

Same pub, same weeknight, two nights. Four weeks is the series if they want you back. The bar is these two nights. Do not start night 2 at a second pub.

Do not invent a drop, a pub, or a headcount. Write the sheet in the pub, the same night. A log counts only when one person paid and another person read the price back. You log last. Empty rows are not people. Write nothing in a row you do not use.

If you are not in the room, do not run it. A mate with a spreadsheet is not the night.

### Before you leave home

- The pub has agreed. You asked them.
- You read the listing this morning. The WhatsApp used that read.
- The mat is in your bag. The back is blank unless you wrote a real link.
- You will be in the room.

### In the room

1. Arrive.
2. Put the mat on the table.
3. Say hello.
4. Phones stay in pockets until a pint is in a hand. The QR, if you have one, is for after that.
5. If two mates came together, split them for the first pint.
6. Name the two roles out loud. The one who pays. The one who reads the price back.
7. Say: you may not order what the person on your left ordered.
8. Say: buy your own.
9. If anyone asks whether it has started, say: "Not started. Nobody has paid yet."
10. The night starts when the first real price is logged. You log your own last.
11. Before people leave, ask each person: "Would you come again?" Write yes or no. One row per person you asked. The counts are those rows. Do not round them. Do not add anyone you did not ask.
12. Fill the log before you leave the pub.

If the room is flat, use the host lines in section 4 the same night. Do not add a screen.

### What good looks like

At least 3 fresh public drops each night, and most people say they would come again.

A fresh public drop is a price someone paid in the room, read back by someone else, and written on this sheet so it can be said out loud. Your private note does not count. Your own log counts only if someone else read it back, and you still log last.

Most people means more yes than no on that night's tally. If you did not ask, you do not have an answer. Do not write "most" without the rows.

## 4. The receipt

Template only. No number that is not a row from that night. If the log is empty, do not send a receipt and do not claim the night happened.

### Next week's message

Build it from the night's log, then add a fresh listing the morning you send it. Read that listing that morning. Do not reuse last week's price as this week's listing. Do not send the plan's £5.40.

Repeat the last-week drink line once for each log. Delete a count line you did not tally. Said yes in this message is the new night, counted from people who replied "I'll be there". Marked here is last night, counted from people who were in the room. No range. No seat count. No "Save me a seat." No "confirmed."

```
Still true? [Pub name], [next date], 8pm.

[Drink] listed [price]. [Source], read [read date].
That listing might be wrong. Tonight we find out.

Last [date], [Pub name]:
[Drink], [price]. [Who paid] paid. [Who read it back] read it back.

[How many said yes] said yes.
[How many marked here] marked here.
Not started. Nobody has paid yet.

Reply "I'll be there" if you're coming.
Buy your own.
Come alone. One pays. One reads the price back.
Not the drink on your left.
```

### Private note

Private by default. Yours, not a post. Pub, date, what you paid, and who drank with you only if they said yes to being named. Ask in the room. A no stays off the note.

```
[Pub name]
[Date]
[Drink]
You paid [price]
With [names who said yes]
```

If you paid for nothing, do not write a price. If nobody said yes to a name, delete the With line.

### Paper for the bar

The same logs, on paper, left on the bar. The prices stay on the paper because they are the logs. Your mouth stays shut about them. No price talk. No ask for money. Put the paper down and leave it. If they pin it, you have their leave to name them again. You still do not name a pub that has not agreed.

Print this, and nothing under it. One block per log.

```
[Pub name]
[Date]

[Drink]
[Price]
Paid by [name]
Read back by [name]
```

### If the first night is flat

Pick one, in the room, the same night. Lines you say. Do not turn them into a screen.

**The clerk.** Before anyone orders, one person reads last week's listed price out loud. Fill it from a listing you read: drink, price, source, read date. Do not use the plan's £5.40.

Say: "Last week's listing was [drink] at [price]. [Source], read [read date]. That is the listing. It is not tonight's price."

The table may answer "as listed", "dearer", or "a steal". Then say: "That's an opinion. The price is what someone pays and someone else reads back."

The opinion never sits where the figure sits. The logged price is the only price.

**The second pub.** A walk, not a second product. Only after the first pub is willing, and only to a pub you have asked. Say: "We walk to the nearest pub we have not logged. If I have not asked them, we stay here."

Do not do this walk until two pubs have said yes. Until then, stay put. Night 2 on this sheet stays at the first pub.
