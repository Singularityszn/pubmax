# Map canvas and pins

Moved from [`components/AGENTS.md`](../../components/AGENTS.md). Authoritative policy detail lives here; the area index stays short.

<a id="a-camera-move-has-an-owner-and-the-map-a-reader-is-looking-at-loads-first"></a>

- **A camera move has an OWNER, and the map a reader is looking at loads first.** The canvas takes ONE `focusPoint` fed by TWO owners, the opening-location answer and the area lane (everything through `moveMapCameraTo`), so the token is an IDENTITY carrying its owner (`lib/mapCameraFocus.ts`), never a bare counter each owner starts at 1. A viewport that names nowhere may not be read from: while the opening question is open the map's placeholder bounds are the WHOLE WORLD, so `openingLoadViewportFor` answers the city's own view, and the ring around a settled viewport waits for idle while the viewport keeps the caller's turn (`VIEWPORT_SHARD_RING` / `NEIGHBOUR_SHARD_RING`). Captain's law: only that map loads, then the sides fill in. The `/map` ceiling in `perf/route-budgets.json` comes DOWN at the next CI sweep, never up. Pins: `__tests__/mapCameraFocus.test.ts`, `__tests__/mapViewportFirstShards.test.ts`.

<a id="nothing-moves-this-camera-but-the-reader-and-there-is-one-compass"></a>

- **Nothing moves this camera but the reader, and there is ONE compass.** Captain 3 Sep 2026: the idle ambient orbit is DELETED, not switched off, because it turned the map at 0.6 degrees a second whenever a reader stopped touching it. While a gesture is on the glass no camera intent runs at all, and for `GESTURE_CAMERA_HOLD_MS` after it only the REACTIVE intents (`route`, `query`, and the arrival turn) stay out, never a move the reader asked for: `lib/mapGestureGuard.ts` is the rule and `scheduleCamera` in `components/map/canvas/useMapCamera.ts` is the one place it is asked. MapLibre's compass is off at the source, because its reset flattened the pitch to a view no city opens on; `lib/mapCompass.ts` owns the one control, which eases BOTH axes back to the city's designed attitude. It lives in the Layers popover from 7 Sep 2026 rather than on the map edge: what that rule refuses is a compass that appears and vanishes with the map's own attitude, and a control the reader opens one button to reach is not that. A phone has no Layers popover, so the same action is `Reset view` in the Layers tab of `More map controls`, handed up by the canvas through `onCameraResetChange`; it is always rendered and disabled while the camera is on the city's attitude. `components/map/canvas/cameraProbe.ts` publishes the camera to the browser suite the way `components/map/canvas/paintedPinProbe.ts` publishes the pins. Pins: `__tests__/mapGestureGuard.test.ts`, `__tests__/mapCompass.test.ts`, `e2e/map-gestures.spec.ts`.

<a id="map-density-is-a-contract-not-a-styling-choice"></a>

- **Map density is a contract, not a styling choice.** The pin and cluster zoom boundaries, the supercluster radius and the symbol-collision policy with its narrow overlap exemptions are the constants at the top of `components/map/canvas/buildScene.ts`, asserted in `__tests__/mapSymbolCollision.test.ts`. Change them there, and keep every app symbol layer inside MapLibre's collision index.

<a id="a-god-component-is-decomposed-in-place-because-the-map-s-own-tests-read-its-sour"></a>

- **A god component is decomposed IN PLACE, because the map's own tests read its SOURCE.** `PubMap` was a 305-branch render function and is 27 (#1185). Roughly 23 test files read `components/PubMap.tsx` as TEXT and assert on its own expressions, so the decomposition adds NO component and leaves that text where it is: pure derivations move to `lib/pubMap.ts`, a JSX subtree becomes a NESTED function inside `PubMap` (ESLint scores complexity per function, and a nested call leaves the React element tree identical where a component would add a fibre), and a derivation whose exact call text is pinned stays a module-scope helper in the component file. The expensive rule to rediscover: React Compiler treats a value handed to a helper it cannot see through as POSSIBLY MUTATED, so wrap such a call in `useMemo` or leave the expression inline, or `preserve-manual-memoization` breaks on the memo blocks near it. Pin: `__tests__/pubMap.test.ts`.

<a id="a-pin-s-colour-may-be-a-hint-a-pin-s-figure-may-not"></a>

- **A pin's COLOUR may be a hint; a pin's FIGURE may not.** Pins print the price itself from `PIN_PRICE_LABEL_MIN_ZOOM` (`components/map/canvas/filters.ts`, which owns the gate and the `text-field` expressions; the tag's size, offset and padding are at the top of `components/map/canvas/buildScene.ts`). The default pint band (`priceBucket`) may be fed by a demo seed so an unpriced city pack still reads as a map; its label is a claim, so `formatPinPriceLabel` in `components/map/canvas/geojson.ts` reads a strictly narrower stack (contributor price, curated `cheapestPrice`, favourite-pint price, never `latestDemoPrice` and never a `priceBand` without a price) and only for pub kinds (`isPubVenueKind`). A selected non-pint lens replaces both band and label from its corroborated category price, names the drink in the label, and makes missing prices neutral. A famous bar, late-food or restaurant venue's default `cheapestPrice` is its anchor price, which printed bare would masquerade as a pint. An uncorroborated report cannot reach either lane. The tag rides `pubs-point` itself and takes the ordinary collision deal (`text-optional`, no `*-allow-overlap`), so where it will not fit the LABEL goes and the pin stays. Pins: `__tests__/mapSymbolCollision.test.ts`, `__tests__/canvas-geojson.test.ts`.

<a id="a-pin-s-fill-says-the-price-its-edge-is-what-makes-it-findable-and-dark-mode-nee"></a>

- **A pin's fill says the price; its EDGE is what makes it findable, and dark mode needs two tones for that.** Dark roads are subordinate warm context in `lib/mapBasemapTaste.ts`, but night ground, water, buildings and road tiers still span distinct tones, so no single rim edges every price band everywhere. `venuePinEdgeTokens` (`components/map/canvas/tokens.ts`) therefore publishes a rim plus an opposite-luminance casing in dark and nothing in light, and `makeVenuePinDraw` in `lib/mapIcons.ts` draws the casing pass first at `GLASS_CASING_STROKE` so the rim can drop to a hairline and the band keeps its area. Do not reach for a brighter band instead: `--brick` is a muted rose because dark `--brass` is a coral, and this map's rings are already spoken for. The UK base pub takes the SAME edge, for the same reason. `__tests__/mapPinBandContrast.test.ts` reads the shipped dark tokens rather than a restated copy and holds every band to an edge over every dark basemap tone.

<a id="the-live-pint-index-arrival-must-full-load-the-map"></a>

- **The live Pint Index arrival must full-load the map.** The area chips in `components/pintindex/PintIndexArrival.tsx` are plain anchors on purpose: the map freezes at mount whether an arrival is intentional (`lib/explicitMapIntent.ts`) and what the camera owes it, so a soft navigation hands it an empty query and opens all of London. They deep-link `?sel=<venue>`, not `?q=<area>`. The arrival funnel's events are in `lib/analyticsEvents.ts` and `docs/METRICS_FUNNEL.md`.

<a id="a-map-that-never-mounts-is-the-shell-s-problem-and-nothing-on-map-may-wait-for-i"></a>

- **A MAP THAT NEVER MOUNTS IS THE SHELL'S PROBLEM, AND NOTHING ON /map MAY WAIT FOR IT FOR EVER.** F08/J33. `components/PubMapCanvas.tsx` diagnoses every way a MOUNTED map dies and each has its own watchdog and honest heading; none can fire when the canvas never mounts, and MapLibre lives in its own chunk behind a dynamic import on purpose. Measured with that one chunk aborted: `/map`, `/map?sel=` and `/map?log=1` all answered with `app/error.tsx`, so a blocked map library took the venue list, the selected pub's sheet and the Pint Drop composer with it. `lib/mapCanvasAvailability.ts` is the whole of the shell's policy and it answers TWO things the canvas cannot: `module` (a `MapCanvasBoundary` in `components/PubMap.tsx` caught the subtree) and `timeout` (`MAP_CANVAS_READINESS_CEILING_MS` lapsed). FOUR rules. (1) The ceiling runs from mount until the canvas reports MapLibre construction, readiness, or its own failure. Construction transfers timeout ownership to the canvas's first-frame, scene-ready, and pin-reveal watchdogs. The loading frame cannot stop the ceiling because shell-owned pins can release that frame without a canvas. (2) RETRY BUILDS A FRESH LAZY: React caches a rejected one for ever, so `pubMapCanvasForAttempt` keys one per attempt and remounting the same component would replay the rejection without touching the network. (3) There is ONE venue view, `components/map/MapFallbackCard.tsx`, shared by the canvas's five failure kinds and the shell's two, and ambient banners stand down while it is up because a road-closure note describes a map the reader cannot see. (4) A CANVAS IS THE ONLY THING THAT REPORTS MAP BOUNDS, and three arrival lanes (search arrival, UK place arrival, national browse) wait for that report before reading a shard: with no canvas `/map?sel=<id>` sat at ZERO venues for the life of the page while a bare `/map` loaded 141, which took the card's rows, the list and the log intent with it, so the shell hands the loader the opening view once per attempt through that same door. Two phone geometry defects rode with it: the fallback card reserved the chrome from `--mobile-map-rail-h`, a property defined NOWHERE, so the `calc()` was invalid at computed-value time and the card's heading sat behind the top bar (it reads `--mobile-map-chrome-full-h` now, published by `components/mobile/mobileMapShell.css`), and the basemap Retry cleared the tab bar alone, so `elementFromPoint` at its own centre answered the plan-activation bar and the tap was swallowed; it reads that stack member's own published berth and height instead. Pins: `__tests__/mapCanvasAvailability.test.ts`, `__tests__/mapSurfaceChrome.test.ts`, `e2e/map-blocked-fallback.spec.ts`; proof `docs/proof/map-blocked-fallback/`.
