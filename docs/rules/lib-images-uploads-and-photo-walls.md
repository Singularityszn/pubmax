# Images, uploads and photo walls

Moved from [`lib/AGENTS.md`](../../lib/AGENTS.md). Authoritative policy detail lives here; the area index stays short.

<a id="a-profile-owns-two-images-and-one-pipeline-and-its-card-fields-are-public-by-cho"></a>

- **A profile owns TWO images and ONE pipeline, and its card fields are public by choice.** `lib/profileImageSlots.ts` holds the closed slot set (`avatar` | `cover`) and everything that differs between them, so a slot is a table row, never a second pipeline: shared journey in `lib/profileImageMedia.server.ts`, owner and flag lanes in `lib/profileImageRoute.server.ts`, public serving in `lib/profileImageServe.server.ts`, thin route files under `app/api/profiles/[handle]/{avatar,cover}` and `app/api/{avatar,cover}`, and `lib/profileStore.ts` mirroring the shape. The card fields are PUBLIC like the bio and the face; `__tests__/profilesRoutePrivacy.test.ts` guards only the private set. What crosses the wire is `PublicProfile` (`lib/profiles.ts`), never `ProfileRecord`, and every image write returns the WHOLE public profile through the ONE projection (`toPublicProfile`, wired by `publicProfileFromRecord`), because a second copy of that field list dropped a founding member's number off an image write. `downloadUploadedImageObject` (`lib/uploadedImage.server.ts`) is the ONE reader behind the avatar, the cover and a wall photo, and `readUploadedImageObject` separates "we could not LOOK" from "this is not an image". Every serve refusal answers the reader the same `Photo not found.`, so each gate names itself once in the log (`profile_image.serve_refused`, `uploaded_image.object_unreadable`). Migration `0096` + rollback. Pins: `__tests__/profileCoverRoute.test.ts`, `__tests__/profileCardFields.test.ts`, `__tests__/profileRichRender.test.ts`, `__tests__/profileImageServeJourney.test.ts`.

<a id="a-backdrop-is-five-photos-on-one-rotation-and-cover-1-is-still-the-cover"></a>

- **A backdrop is FIVE photos on one rotation, and cover #1 is still the cover.** Pure policy in `lib/profileCovers.ts` (cap, hold, order, `moveCoverPosition`, `profileCoverUrls`, `coverCarouselRotates`); rows in `profile_cover_photos` (migration `0100` + rollback, apply before deploying) behind `lib/profileCoverPhotoStore.ts`; handlers in `lib/profileCoverPhotoRoute.server.ts` under `app/api/profiles/[handle]/covers`, reusing the entire cover pipeline per photo. FOUR rules: `profiles.cover_*` stays the back-compat mirror of position 1; only `generation_mismatch` and `image_absent` may ask the rotation for another generation through `ProfileImageServeDeps.extraServingKey` (`ROTATION_MAY_ANSWER` in `lib/profileImageServe.server.ts`), every other refusal being terminal and a profile-level hide covering all five; `PublicProfile.coverUrls` absent means "not asked", never "no covers"; reduced motion means NO rotation, and the other four are never fetched. `lib/profileCoverModeration.server.ts` is the ONE place a cover decision crosses both stores, and a RESTORE with no mirror image refuses. A reorder upserts id, profile and position ALONE (`toReorderRow`), because replaying held rows resurrected covers a moderator had hidden, and a reorder whose owner guard reads `unavailable` after the write hides the rotation before it rethrows. `Remove cover` picks its lane ONCE (`profileCoverRemoveLane`) over a FOUR-WAY read (`ProfileCoverReadState`) shared by the card, the status line and the control, so "empty" and "could not read" cannot merge; the editor's held covers are DERIVED from the profile prop, never state; an image write reports through `onProfileChanged`, never `onSaved`, which closes the editor. The band's display aspect equals the cropper's (`profileImageSlotSpec.cover.aspectRatio`), and the crop styling is `components/profile/profileImageCropper.css`, imported by `ProfileImageCropper`, so the DM thread and the profile page cannot drift; `app/u/[handle]/profile.css` keeps only the face's square, its mask and one zoom-slider override. Pins: `__tests__/profileCovers.test.ts`, `__tests__/profileCoverTakedown.test.ts`, `__tests__/profileCoverPhotosRoute.test.ts`, `__tests__/profileCoverCarousel.test.ts`, `__tests__/profileCoverEditorLanes.test.ts`, `__tests__/profileEditorHeldCovers.test.ts`, `e2e/profile-hero-desktop.spec.ts`, `e2e/profile-photo-crop.spec.ts`.

<a id="storage-takes-a-blob-and-a-write-proves-itself"></a>

- **Storage takes a Blob, and a write proves itself.** `@supabase/storage-js` builds a multipart body for a Blob and ONLY for a Blob; every other body is handed to the runtime's `fetch` raw, and on Vercel that string-decoded our JPEG while still answering 200. So `uploadUploadedImageObject` in `lib/uploadedImage.server.ts` is the ONE writer behind the avatar, the cover, a wall photo and a feed photo: never call `.storage.from(...).upload(...)` with a Buffer. Promotion reads its own serving key back through the shared reader (`proveUploadedImageWrite`) and refuses with `PROCESSING_FAILED`, deleting the object. That proof is TRI-STATE: only bytes we DID read and that are not ours are `corrupt`, and a read we could not run is `unproven` and costs an owner nothing. Pin: `__tests__/profileImageServeJourney.test.ts`, whose fake bucket mangles a non-Blob body exactly as the runtime did.

<a id="a-new-price-comes-with-the-bill-and-the-pint-photo-is-the-fun-one"></a>

- **A NEW PRICE COMES WITH THE BILL, AND THE PINT PHOTO IS THE FUN ONE.** Captain 7 Sept 2026: "whenever a person is submitting a new price, they have to take a picture of the bill", and at 17:20 "the bill photo should be mandatory, along with the picture of the pint or something, just to make it more fun". `lib/pintDropReceipt.ts` is the whole rule and every word of it: `priceNeedsReceipt` (only a PRICE is owed one, so a note, a memory or a pub photo is asked for nothing), `photoRefusal` (the browser half of the type and 4 MB rules, quoted from `lib/uploadBodyLimit.ts`), `RECEIPT_REQUIRED_LINE`, `RECEIPT_PHOTO_LABEL` and `PINT_PHOTO_FUN_LINE`. Both write doors REFUSE a priced write without it (`POST /api/pint-drops`, `POST /api/price-submit`), before their rate limits, so a browser is never the gate. FOUR rules ride with it. (1) The bill is a THIRD photo slot, `receipt_photo_key` (migration `0153` + rollback), never a reuse of the pint or venue slot, and the store's additive-rollout guard saves the drop without its key until the migration is applied. (2) A bad FILE is the drinker's to fix and answers 400; a Storage outage is OURS and may not cost the price, so the drop lands with no receipt key and the loss is logged. Both doors tell the two apart by the CLASS, `PhotoRefusalError`, never by the message: they used to ask whether it started with "Photo must", so `UNREADABLE_PHOTO_REFUSAL`, the one refusal worded differently, came back as a retryable 503 that invited a drinker to send the same bytes again. (3) The composer asks in order: the bill with its one line, then the playful `PINT_PHOTO_FUN_LINE` in that line's place, and Log it stays disabled until the bill is in and says why. (4) The ANONYMOUS path is untouched (the 4 Sep ruling): a receipt is evidence about a price, never a second identity. Pins: `__tests__/pintDropReceipt.test.ts`, `__tests__/pintDrops.test.ts`, `__tests__/priceSubmitRoute.test.ts`, `e2e/price-bill-required.spec.ts`, `e2e/helpers/priceBill.ts`; proof `docs/proof/price-two-drinkers-receipt/`.

<a id="a-photo-on-the-wire-weighs-at-most-4-mb-and-the-composer-fits-it-there"></a>

- **A PHOTO ON THE WIRE WEIGHS AT MOST 4 MB, AND THE COMPOSER FITS IT THERE.** Every upload route is a Vercel function, and the platform refuses a body over 4.5 MB with a plain-text 413 FUNCTION_PAYLOAD_TOO_LARGE before any handler runs, so a server cap of 10 MB was a promise no route could keep (contribution battle test D02, 5 Sep 2026: an 8 MB phone JPEG answered 413 on the Moment and wall routes alike). `lib/uploadBodyLimit.ts` is the ONE number, a pure leaf the browser and the server both read; `UPLOADED_IMAGE_MAX_BYTES` and `MOMENT_MAX_PHOTO_BYTES` read it, and `__tests__/uploadBodyLimit.test.ts` holds the figure under the platform's limit and checks that the browser (`photoRefusal`) and the store (`validatePhoto`) both take a photo at the cap and refuse one a byte over it. `uploadPhotoSizeLabel` joins the figure to its unit with a no-break space, so a wrap never parts them. The Moment composer's intake is `lib/momentPhotoIntake.ts` (pure) plus `lib/momentPhotoFit.ts` (the canvas): a file is admitted by its LEADING BYTES, never its name (D05: a text file with a jpg extension arrives typed as a JPEG), a JPEG, PNG or WebP under the cap goes up byte for byte, and anything heavier or any HEIC is decoded and re-encoded down a ladder with the editor's own encoder until it fits. The Moment picker is swept by `__tests__/profilePhotoPicker.test.ts` like every other picker (D03: it carried `capture` and no HEIC). A refused Moment write keeps the draft's Memory id unless the route answers `NIGHT_MEMORY_REFUSED` or 404 (`keepServerMemoryAfterRefusal`), because every 400 used to mint a fresh Memory per retry. Pins: `__tests__/momentPhotoIntake.test.ts`, `__tests__/momentPhotoFit.test.ts`, `__tests__/momentCaptureIntake.test.tsx`; proof `docs/proof/photo-upload-limits/`.

<a id="choosing-a-profile-photo-is-two-beats-and-the-first-one-is-a-picker-never-a-came"></a>

- **Choosing a profile photo is TWO BEATS, and the first one is a PICKER, never a camera.** `capture` on a file input instructs iOS to open the camera and leave Photo Library out of the sheet, so no file input under `components/profile` or `app/u` may carry it. No browser test reproduces that, so the fence is the source: `__tests__/profilePhotoPicker.test.ts`. `lib/profileImagePicker.ts` owns the rest and is pure: the ONE `accept` both slots share, which must name HEIC and HEIF; the crop geometry, because it decides which pixels leave a person's phone; and the friction copy. The second beat is `components/profile/ProfileImageCropper.tsx`, whose JPEG is what makes an iPhone's HEIC uploadable, so widening the picker never widens `ALLOWED_TYPES` in `lib/profileImageMedia.server.ts`. A browser that cannot decode the file says where to go and sends nothing. The upload routes, the multipart `photo` field and the safety scan are untouched by design. The hidden input stays a 1px transparent box, never `display: none`. Pins: `__tests__/profileImagePicker.test.ts`, `e2e/profile-photo-crop.spec.ts` (also on the Safari engine under `PW_WEBKIT_PROFILE_PHOTO_CROP=1`).

<a id="the-safety-scan-on-an-owned-image-is-advisory-and-the-moderator-lane-is-the-net"></a>

- **The safety scan on an owned image is ADVISORY, and the moderator lane is the net.** `lib/uploadedImageScan.server.ts` is the ONE policy every owned-image surface shares. A VERDICT is honoured, so refused bytes never reach a serving key. Every other outcome is a fact about US rather than about the photo, so the upload PROCEEDS as `approved` and the skip is one quiet `uploaded_image.scan_skipped` warn line naming the surface and the reason. Captain decision 2026-08-10, after a provider fault made every avatar, cover and wall upload answer 503: `MODERATION_UNAVAILABLE` is gone from these routes, and do not reintroduce a 503 for an unreachable scanner. Reporting and hiding take a bad image down and are untouched. `DEFAULT_OPENROUTER_MODERATION_MODEL` in `lib/profileAvatarModeration.ts` is a model id, and a wrong one makes every scan a 4xx. Pins: `__tests__/uploadedImageScan.test.ts`, `__tests__/profileAvatarRoute.test.ts`, `__tests__/profileCoverRoute.test.ts`, `__tests__/venuePhotosRoute.test.ts`.

<a id="a-pub-photo-wall-is-a-photo-wall-and-a-crosspost-is-a-promise"></a>

- **A pub photo wall is a photo wall, and a crosspost is a promise.** Every venue carries a community wall, and `lib/venuePhotos.ts` owns the policy: the per-account per-venue cap, an optional tag from the one drink taxonomy, an optional caption, portrait crop. It is never a price lane: nothing on a wall reaches `mergeCommunityPriceSignals`, pin colour, cheapest buckets or the Pint Index. The bytes take the SAME journey the owned profile images take, extracted to `lib/uploadedImage.server.ts` so the asserted EXIF strip cannot drift between them: staging, signed URL, the scan adapter, and promotion unless the scan REFUSED. The cap counts LIVE rows, so a moderator hide gives the slot back; reporting queues and never hides; hiding never deletes and keeps its own lane. The author is stored as the stable `profile:{uuid}` actor and printed through the ONE public projection (`publicProfileFromRecord`), so a handle rename never strands a photo. "Also share to your feed" answers on a three-way scale (`VenuePhotoCrosspostState`), never a boolean: the feed write goes through the existing Social post machinery behind `requireVerifiedSocialActor`, so the wall buys no lane around Social's own gate, and a feed failure is reported rather than failing the wall the photo already reached. Migration `0098` repeats the object-key shape, the taxonomy and the actor binding, and extends the tombstone trigger to take an author's wall photos and their bytes with them. The city Drink Wall (`/wall`, policy in `lib/drinkWall.ts`) is the same `venue_photos` row with a wall category (`pint`, `pub`, `london`) and an optional pub link (migration `0158`), so one report, hide or delete takes a photo off every wall it is on; its boundaries are its entry in `docs/WRITE_SURFACE_CERTIFICATION.md`. Pins: `__tests__/venuePhotos*.test.ts`, `__tests__/drinkWall*`, `e2e/venue-photo-wall.spec.ts`, `e2e/drink-wall.spec.ts`.
