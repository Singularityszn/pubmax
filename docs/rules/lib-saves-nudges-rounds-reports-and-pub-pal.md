<a id="a-visit-report-is-an-account-of-one-visit-never-a-venue-rating"></a>

Public reads carry `ready` or `degraded`, because a failed lookup may never read as no visits, and `/api/visit-reports?contributor=<handle>` is the leaderboard count seam. Reader flags only queue a row, only a moderator hides it, hiding never deletes its provenance, and a hidden row keeps its own moderator lane (`GET ?status=hidden`). <a id="a-crowd-occupancy-report-is-a-now-reading-about-seats-never-a-remembered-night"></a>

- **A crowd occupancy report is a NOW reading about seats, never a remembered night.** `lib/occupancy.ts` owns the three buttons, the now and retake windows and the explicit map onto Visit Report busyness, so the two tenses cannot invent a fourth word. Trust is derived on READ: only reports inside the now window may answer now, older rows stay for the forecast and never paint a now surface, and the printed reporter figure counts DISTINCT accounts, never rows. That route also owns the flag and hide pair (migration `0109` + rollback): reporting never auto-hides, hide never deletes, and `report_count` is a DISTINCT reporter count, so `venue_occupancy_flags.actor_hash` is NOT NULL with an `anonymous` sentinel. Either deploy order is safe, because the store drops to 0107's columns on a PostgREST 42703 rather than 503-ing every tap. <a id="a-round-records-spend-never-debt"></a>

- **A Round records spend, never debt.** `lib/rounds.ts` owns immutable buying turns and derives rotation from their payer order. A plain total stays inside the Round diary. Itemised drink lines reach the community-price store only for a signed-in account with a claimed public handle; anonymous lines stay private in the diary. Membership resolves through stable profile ownership, so a handle rename cannot strand a member or retry. Each line persists its promotion state, so dependency failures retry idempotently and only `promoted` lines claim community-price status; the latest line from one account for each venue and drink category owns that key, and earlier same-key lines become `superseded`, stay in the diary and stop claiming live status. Older rows without durable outcome evidence stay neutral as `legacy_unknown`, never relabelled. Promoted lines can affect map price surfaces only through `mergeCommunityPriceSignals`. Never add balances, shares, IOUs, settlement or debt totals to this model. A drink line is a first-party observation, so a known price may only PRE-FILL the composer's drink row, and the remaining gate on what reaches `submitCommunityPrice` is PROVENANCE, never the figure: `RoundSpendItemSource` records whether a line is the drinker's own claim or a figure lifted off the seeded demo menu, and a demo line stays in the diary labelled rather than dropped. Value equality is the wrong fence, because a drinker who really paid what a demo menu quotes is still observing it. Pin: `__tests__/roundsRoute.test.ts`.

<a id="the-voice-allowance-is-metered-by-the-server-never-by-the-browser"></a>

- **The voice allowance is metered by the server, never by the browser.** Migration `0176` + rollback (the captain applies it; a deploy ahead of it answers 503 and the browser falls back to text). Before it, `POST /api/pub-pal/voice-token` with `action: "release"` billed the `durationSeconds` the browser sent, so a caller who sent zero was billed nothing and could start sessions for ever (14 of 14 sessions answered 200 with `used_minutes` 0). FOUR rules. (1) ISSUING A URL PREPAYS THE WHOLE CAP: `prepay_pub_pal_voice_grant` charges `PAL_VOICE_GRANT_MINUTES` (3, derived from `PAL_VOICE_MAX_SESSION_SECONDS`) in the same upsert that tests the month, and admits a session while `used_minutes` is under `PAL_VOICE_MONTHLY_MINUTES`, so ten simultaneous sessions are admitted and an eleventh is `VOICE_ALLOWANCE_USED`. (2) NOTHING THE BROWSER SAYS MOVES THE METER: the release body is read for ONE field, the `conversationId` it was handed, and `durationSeconds` is ignored. The provider is asked about that conversation only after `owns_issued_pub_pal_voice_conversation` says the caller holds an issued, unsettled grant for it, so a made-up id costs one database read and never a provider request. Settling reads the duration ElevenLabs recorded for that conversation (`lib/pubPalVoiceProviderDuration.server.ts`, only once the call has `processing`, `done` or `failed` status) and `settle_pub_pal_voice_conversation` refunds the unused part of the prepayment under the CALLER'S OWN grant, once; a call that ran past the cap charges the difference. The browser releases as it hangs up, while ElevenLabs may still report the call running, so the release asks the provider again once a second for at most five seconds (`providerCallSecondsOnceEnded`). A grant the release could not settle stays `issued`, and the owner's next `POST` settles every `issued` grant of that month (`issued_pub_pal_voice_conversations`) from one provider read each, BEFORE `prepay_pub_pal_voice_grant` tests the allowance. A grant ElevenLabs never reports as ended, a provider that cannot be read, a missing duration or a second release leaves the grant charged in full: every failure is the safe direction. No cron and no webhook settle a grant. (3) ONLY THE SERVER CAN REFUND A GRANT, AND ONLY BEFORE A URL LEFT: `refund_pub_pal_voice_grant` is service-role only and refuses a settled or already refunded grant, and it takes a `reserved` grant and an `issued` one, because a link that committed with its reply lost leaves the grant issued while the route returns 503 and no URL. The route refunds only on its own failure (provider error, no conversation id, bind or link failure, an ambiguous RPC reply) and never after it hands a URL out. (4) THE OLD FUNCTIONS ARE CLOSED: `consume_pub_pal_voice_trial`, `release_pub_pal_voice_trial` and `record_pub_pal_voice_minutes` are revoked from `service_role`, so a mixed-version deploy cannot reach the client-trusted path. The keyless in-memory meter prepays and refunds the same way and settles nothing. The rollback reopens the client-trusted meter, so the application goes back first. Pins: `__tests__/pubPalVoiceGrantsMigrationEffective.test.ts` (real PostgreSQL 16: the fault on the pre-0176 catalog, the route end to end over the real functions, the settle, the concurrency, the closed grants, then the rollback), `__tests__/pubPalVoiceTokenRoute.test.ts`, `__tests__/pubPalVoice.test.tsx`.

Each page wears its area's landmark, the map's own pictogram read from the map sprite (`palPassportLandmark`: Westminster the clock tower, Southwark the Shard, and a steady one for every other area), and the Pal in a pose of its own for that area (`palPassportPose`), and nothing counts pages or areas. FIVE rules. (1) THE QUEST IS THE MISSION: it is the price evidence mission `/near` already ranks and answers through `VenuePriceSubmit` (see the mission rule in `docs/rules/lib-prices-trust-and-drinks.md`); the Pal adds only its words (`palQuestLine` in `lib/palQuest.ts`, from the mission's own reason, and never more than that reason holds: "log a price at", "is out of date", "check the price at", never who logged one or that nobody did) and the stamp, never a second ranking, task or write. (2) A PRICE, NEVER A DRINK: every quest says "No need to buy anything. Read the board or ask at the bar.", and no line counts drinks, asks for a round or names a pace. (3) A STAMP IS A MARK, NEVER A SCORE: a stamp is the pub and the London day the quest was answered (`stampPalQuest`), one a day however many prices follow, drawn as the Pal's own print in an inked ring; the book shows no total, no streak and no level (`docs/VOICE.md`, `gamified-level`), and nothing in the product branches on a stamp. (4) IT STAYS ON THE DEVICE AND LEAVES WITH THE ACCOUNT: the book is `localStorage` only, at most sixty stamps, under a device identity key (`DEVICE_IDENTITY_LOCAL_KEYS` in `lib/deviceAccountIdentity.ts`), and nothing is sent. (5) ONLY THE SIGNED-IN OWNER'S PAL ASKS: the stored Pal counts only for the account that owns it, and a hidden Pal is no Pal (`components/pal/useOwnPal.ts`); with no Pal, another account's Pal, or a day already stamped, the mission shows as it always did. The quest state belongs to the Pal's owner while `/near` stays open: an account switch starts it afresh, the book is read again when another tab changes it, and `stampPalQuest` returns only a stamp it added, so an answer in a second tab never shows the first tab's pub as its own (`__tests__/nearPalQuest.test.tsx`). A muted Pal is still, and the jump and the stamp's landing play only under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`.
