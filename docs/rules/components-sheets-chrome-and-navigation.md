# Sheets, chrome and navigation

Moved from [`components/AGENTS.md`](../../components/AGENTS.md). Authoritative policy detail lives here; the area index stays short.

<a id="no-surface-is-a-dead-end-and-the-way-out-is-one-pattern"></a>

- **No surface is a dead end, and the way out is one pattern.** `lib/surfaceStack.ts` holds the presentation-free trail. On the Map, `components/map/pubmap/useMapSurfaceNavigation.ts` is the only browser-history owner: every entry carries the complete trail plus venue selection state, direct opens publish through `commitOpen` before React changes drawers, and opens that land before the owner's first layout init queue on `pendingOpensRef` and flush in that same init pass so a deep-linked `sel=` is not dropped while history restores, and its one `popstate` listener restores the landed snapshot. An internal open may only `pushState` or `replaceState`; reopening a surface already lower in the trail writes a fresh entry with the current surface as its parent and never calls `history.go()`. `components/ui/surface-nav.tsx` is the only affordance: leading Back, trailing Home, the same box in the same two cells of one head, on the phone sheet, desktop drawers and List view. Back renders only when there is a parent; Escape is keyboard Back, fling-dismiss is gesture Back, and a tap on an open overlay's own phone top-bar control is Back too, never a bare close that leaves the surface on the trail. A phone sheet MOUNTS only where its shell owns the lane (`sheetsEnabled`, `components/mobile/MobileMapShell.tsx`), because a mounted sheet still claims Escape on `window` and still captures focus, and CSS reaches neither. The sheet also OWNS ITS OWN STYLESHEET: `components/mobile/MobileSharedSheet.tsx` imports `components/mobile/mobileMapShell.css` itself, because PubMap renders the phone sheet through a static import while the shell that used to be the stylesheet's only importer is loaded dynamically, and on a `/map?sel=` arrival at 390 the venue sheet stood unstyled (`position: static`, `z-index: auto`) for the one to four seconds before the shell chunk landed (#1490). While `mapSelectionFrame` still waits on the slim index for a deep-linked `sel=`, `detailOpen` stays true and `PubMap` mounts `VenueSheetSkeleton` until the venue resolves. Back must restore exact held state, never reload a default, and never push history from inside a setState updater. A panel anchored to a visible trigger is not in the trail and owes only `lib/useDismissOnEscape.ts` plus outside dismiss; ambient banners and chips owe neither. Pins: `__tests__/mapSurfaceHistory.test.ts`, `__tests__/surfaceStack.test.ts`, `__tests__/mapShellSheetOwnership.test.ts`, `__tests__/pubMap.test.ts` (`mapSelectionFrame`), `e2e/map-surface-history.spec.ts`, `e2e/surface-back-and-home.spec.ts`.

<a id="a-map-control-beside-the-venue-drawer-is-an-exempt-surface-never-a-hole-in-the-t"></a>

- **A MAP CONTROL BESIDE THE VENUE DRAWER IS AN EXEMPT SURFACE, NEVER A HOLE IN THE TRAP.** Captain 13 Sep 2026 (lane 5): the desktop drawer's `map-surface` focus trap inerted the whole map stage section, so the mapped-route chip's `Check last train at final stop` took no click or Enter, and the chip sat under the drawer's left edge at 1440. A control that must stay live while the drawer is open carries `data-focus-trap-exempt` (`FOCUS_TRAP_EXEMPT_ATTRIBUTE`, `lib/useFocusTrap.ts`). THREE rules. (1) Inert is inherited, so the trap walks DOWN a sibling that holds an exempt surface and inerts only the children beside its path (`inertTargets`), re-derived while the trap holds, because adding a second stop from the drawer mounts the chip. (2) Tab from the drawer's last control reaches the exempt surface and returns (`nextTrapFocus`), and the drawer drops `aria-modal` while the chip is live; when an exempt surface leaves with focus inside it (the chip's `Hide`), focus returns to the drawer's first control unless a strict modal is active; a `strict-modal` trap exempts nothing. Shared ownership and suspension follow the contract in `lib/useFocusTrap.ts`. (3) An exempt chip takes the map lane the drawer leaves from 769px, where the drawer is a side panel, at its full 44px buttons; from 641px to 768px the drawer is a full-width bottom sheet with no lane, so the chip leaves the layout while it is open (`components/map/mapBannerStaging.css`). Pins: `__tests__/focusTrapExemptSurface.test.ts`, `e2e/drawer-route-chip-reachable.spec.ts`.

<a id="a-landmark-story-lives-in-the-frame-each-width-already-owns-and-the-canvas-only-"></a>

- **A LANDMARK STORY LIVES IN THE FRAME EACH WIDTH ALREADY OWNS, and the canvas only reports the tap.** Captain 2026-09-05, from a phone at 07:41: the story was a card `PubMapCanvas` pinned over the map, so on a phone its title sat under the chip row, pins showed through its edges and its last rows hid under the planning pill and the dock, while at 768 and 1440 it sat under the toolbar and the banners. `PubMap` owns the selection (synced to `?landmark=` as before), and ONE body, `components/map/LandmarkStoryBody.tsx`, is rendered in two frames: the phone's shared `Sheet` as kind `landmark` (the landmark's name is the chrome's one heading, so the body never repeats it) and the desktop's LEFT drawer, the planner's frame, so the toolbar and the ambient banners leave its lane the way they leave the planner's (`story-open` beside `planning-open` in `components/map/mapToolbar.css` and `components/map/mapBannerStaging.css`, and `MapToolbar` takes `storyOpen` because its lane is a SPRING that reads the open drawer's width, never CSS alone). A landmark pick leaves every other surface the way a pin tap does, and a venue pick retires the story ON SCREEN while the trail keeps it: the story is a `landmark` entry in `useMapSurfaceNavigation` (state `landmarkId`, title the landmark's name), `coordinatedMapOverlay` answers `landmark` under the venue and the planner, so a pub opened out of the story offers `Back to Covent Garden` and the browser's Back restores the story (verify-preview-4 J02), and the phone's planning pill is not painted under the sheet. FOUR rules ride with it. (1) A Wikimedia hero is asked through Special:FilePath and the browser follows its redirect chain, so EVERY host that chain lands on is in the CSP `img-src` (the thumb host for a sized file, measured 2026-09-05); a host missing there draws a broken-image glyph with the credit bar still under it. (2) A failed photo swaps the whole figure for the brand treatment and the credit leaves with it (`components/LandmarkHeroPhoto.tsx`), because a credit is a claim about the bytes on screen. (3) The story says where it is (`lib/landmarkArea.ts`, `In Piccadilly & Soho`) rather than the top bar being rewritten, because the bar prints the area the reader CHOSE; only an area whose own radius covers the point may claim the landmark. (4) A nearby distance prints through `formatLogNearbyDistance` alone and the straight-line caveat is said ONCE in the section head (`STORY_PUBS_DISTANCE_CAVEAT`), on the map and on `/landmark/[id]` alike. Pins: `__tests__/landmarkHeroPhoto.test.tsx`, `__tests__/landmarkArea.test.ts`, `__tests__/pubMapCanvasDistanceContract.test.ts`, `__tests__/clerkProxyCsp.test.ts`, `e2e/landmark-story-sheet.spec.ts`; proof `docs/proof/story-sheet-ux/`.

<a id="a-sheet-says-how-much-of-itself-is-off-screen-once-and-the-shell-cannot-be-scrol"></a>

- **A SHEET SAYS HOW MUCH OF ITSELF IS OFF SCREEN, ONCE, AND THE SHELL CANNOT BE SCROLLED.** UI sweep 5 Sep 2026 (#1516). At 641-768 the inline map drawer is a viewport-tall bottom sheet translated down by its snap, so 45dvh of its scrollport hangs under the screen at `half`. The venue command bar restated that translate as a dvh inset, and the map shell (class `appShell`) was `overflow: hidden`, which is still a scroll container: one `scrollIntoView` on a price row scrolled the shell 403px, the drawer's box moved, the inset did not, and the bar sat mid-viewport over "Published price". THREE rules. (1) The shell is `overflow: clip`, so nothing can scroll it. (2) `SpringDrawer` publishes the px it translates by as `--drawer-sheet-offset`, and the 768 band in `app/globals.css` derives `--sheet-hidden-below` from it ONCE (the per-snap dvh declarations beside the transforms are the fallback for the frame before the spring's first) and ends the scroll body with an `::after` spacer of that height so the last row is reachable. A spacer, never padding, because Chromium measures a sticky inset from the scroller's CONTENT edge. (3) A bottom-pinned child reads that variable for its inset, and the scroll container declares its viewing region with `scroll-padding-bottom` (the hidden part plus the bar), because `scrollIntoView` and a focus scroll aim at the scrollport and would otherwise land a row under the bar. Pins: `e2e/venue-sheet-tablet-command-bar.spec.ts`; proof `docs/proof/venue-sheet-768/`.

<a id="the-venue-types-are-one-control-at-every-width-and-the-count-rides-the-closed-on"></a>

- **THE VENUE TYPES ARE ONE CONTROL AT EVERY WIDTH, AND THE COUNT RIDES THE CLOSED ONE.** PlanAstra item 9, over the map's chrome overload at 768 and 1440: the kind chips FLOATED over the desktop map as a permanent band from 641px up, part of the 20 to 24 controls a tablet met before it had tapped a pin. They are now the contents of ONE `Filters` control in the toolbar row (`components/map/MapVenueKindFilter.tsx`), which the reader opens, and the 7 Sep walk moved the two other controls that narrow the same pin set in beside them: `Show me` (the experience lens) and the fare-zone picker. FOUR rules. (1) NOTHING BELOW 641px MOVED: a phone still reads the same toggles in its Filters sheet, and the desktop control is `display: none` there, so a viewport still gets ONE home for them and never two (design judgement 2026-08-01, finding 2.3). (2) THE POPOVER IS A VIEW OF THE MAP'S OWN FILTER, never a second copy: same `venueKindVisibility` state, same defaults, same lens round trip, and closing the panel writes nothing. (3) A CLOSED PANEL MAY NOT HIDE WHICH KINDS THE MAP IS LEAVING OUT, the rule "Show me" and "Drink" already keep beside it, so the control reads `Filters · 2` and its accessible name says what the 2 counts; `lib/venueKindFilters.ts` owns that word AND the set it counts (`offeredVenueKinds`), because a badge counting a kind the open panel does not offer would claim a filter nobody can reach - the food view offers two kinds, so it counts two. The count is `mapFilterRefinementCount` and it covers EVERY refinement the panel holds, kinds plus lens plus zone, for the same reason: one of three counted would say the map is unfiltered while two filters are on. A CHIP NOBODY CAN PRESS IS NOT A CHIP: `Clubs` sat there permanently `aria-disabled`, explained by a `title` no phone shows, and it could never be enabled, so it is deleted rather than kept as a promise. (4) THE CHIPS COST THE COLD MAP NOTHING: they are a dynamic import inside the panel, so the map's first paint carries one button. Pins: `__tests__/mapVenueKindFilter.test.ts`, `e2e/map-desktop-filters.spec.ts`, `e2e/desktop-map-rail.spec.ts`; proof `docs/proof/map-desktop-filters/`.

FOUR rules. (1) THE ARRIVAL STRIP MAY NOT MAKE THE MAP INERT; its `interactionLocked` lock is deleted from `components/PubMap.tsx`, `components/PubMapCanvas.tsx` and `components/mobile/MobileMapShell.tsx`, not switched off. (2) On a phone the ask is a compact pill, the question, `Use my location` and the close on one row with the location sentence as a small visible line under them, parked one gap above the map-edge column's published top edge, because a top strip still covered the upper map on first open; from 641px it is a strip under the toolbar capped by `--map-arrival-strip-max-h` (`components/map/mapArrivalCard.css`). It is NOT a member of the floating bottom stack (`components/nav/mobileNav.css`), and its primary is `--color-accent`, one button family. (3) THE READER'S OWN FIRST MOVE IS THE ANSWER: a gesture or a pin tap calls `dismissMapFirstVisitArrivalOnMapUse`, from PubMap, on a reader's gesture only. (4) ONE BANNER AT A TIME and the strip is the first of them, so the closure banner, the conditions-and-area-news rail, the concierge ask and the Pub Pal chip all wait (`components/map/mapBannerStaging.css`, gated in JS by `showMapArrivalCard`). The Pub Pal chip still waits for the strip alone. A PANEL the reader opens from the toolbar takes the surface back, which covers an OPEN Tonight panel - the reader opened it, or a `/map?src=whats-on-*` deep link opened it for them, so the two banners above it yield to IT rather than hiding it - and the strip hides while any `[aria-expanded="true"]` control in the toolbar is open, because the Filters popover opened at x 503 to 863 straight through a strip spanning 380 to 1060 and left it painted either side, read as a card cut in three. <a id="the-map-arrives-with-one-slight-turn-and-then-holds-still"></a>

- **THE MAP ARRIVES WITH ONE SLIGHT TURN, AND THEN HOLDS STILL.** Captain 7 Sep 2026: "I also want the map to rotate slightly." Measured first: the shipped map arrives FLAT, bearing 0 and pitch 0, because the canvas is built while the opening-location question is still open and takes the hold view's attitude, and the focus move that follows carries centre and zoom only. `lib/mapArrivalBearing.ts` is the whole rule and a pure leaf: ease 0 to 4 degrees over one second, once per mounted map, then nothing. FOUR cases make no move at all - reduced motion, a deep link that named a pub or a landmark, a bearing somebody already owns, and a bearing that is not a number. It is NOT the idle orbit returning: `arrivalBearingSpentRef` spends it once, because `mapInstanceReady` is set once on first mount and never changes, preventing the arrival bearing effect from restarting across WebGL context rebuilds. It rides the one camera lane as a REACTIVE intent, so a reader holding the map keeps their own attitude (`lib/mapGestureGuard.ts`). TWO further facts, both measured on 7 Sep 2026. (1) THE TURN WAITS FOR THE MAP TO STOP, and the window lives in the rule leaf because the browser spec waits out the same window: without the wait the opening-location answer cancelled the turn's pending frame on the latest-wins camera lane, and with the spec keeping its own shorter copy it read the bearing DURING the wait and reported a flat map on a build that turns in 3.9 seconds. (2) THE MAP ARRIVES AT ONE OF TWO ATTITUDES and the app does not pick which: flat 0 when the canvas is built while the opening-location question is open, or the city's own designed bearing (London -8) when that question answers first. A flat map turns and an attitude is kept, so the map is off north either way; a browser spec may assert THAT, and only the unit test may assert the four.

<a id="phone-chrome-is-measured-not-eyeballed-and-a-trust-caption-never-ellipses"></a>

- **Phone chrome is measured, not eyeballed, and a trust caption never ellipses.** Phone rows share the balanced outer boundary published in `components/mobile/mobileMapShell.css`; the Tonight Arc reserves the `--mobile-map-corner-lane` inside it so the fixed TfL control and a right safe-area inset cannot steal its taps. `e2e/mobile-map-chrome-fit.spec.ts` owns the rendered 320, 390 and 430 geometry, horizontal scrolling, centre-point hit ownership, the map chrome tap-target floors and the budget guarantees; `e2e/price-caption-integrity.spec.ts` owns the rule that the venue price caption wraps rather than truncating. Do the measuring in a real device viewport: `chrome-devtools-axi open` drops the CDP device override, so `open`, then `emulate`, then reload in place, because a second `open` resets it and the mobile shell only mounts if the page booted at phone width. A truncated price caption cuts the meaning the whole trust argument rests on: widen the cell or wrap the line, never ellipsis it. The phone tab bar ALSO answers to the on-screen keyboard, which no phone browser shrinks the layout viewport for. `lib/softKeyboard.ts` is the ONE rule and it needs BOTH halves, a focused text field AND a visual viewport that has lost `SOFT_KEYBOARD_MIN_SHRINK_RATIO` of the layout one, because a caret alone is a physical keyboard and a shrink alone is the URL bar. It hides by TRANSFORM in the open-sheet idiom, never by dropping the reserved bottom clearance, or the page reflows under the caret; a focus recompute is deferred by a task, because `focusout` fires before the next field takes focus. Pins: `__tests__/softKeyboard*.test.ts`.
