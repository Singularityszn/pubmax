<a id="no-surface-is-a-dead-end-and-the-way-out-is-one-pattern"></a>

- **No surface is a dead end, and the way out is one pattern.** `lib/surfaceStack.ts` holds the presentation-free trail. On the Map, `components/map/pubmap/useMapSurfaceNavigation.ts` is the only browser-history owner: every entry carries the complete trail plus venue selection state, direct opens publish through `commitOpen` before React changes drawers, and opens that land before the owner's first layout init queue on `pendingOpensRef` and flush in that same init pass so a deep-linked `sel=` is not dropped while history restores, and its one `popstate` listener restores the landed snapshot. An internal open may only `pushState` or `replaceState`; reopening a surface already lower in the trail writes a fresh entry with the current surface as its parent and never calls `history.go()`. `components/ui/surface-nav.tsx` is the only affordance: leading Back, trailing Home, the same box in the same two cells of one head, on the phone sheet, desktop drawers and List view. Back renders only when there is a parent; Escape is keyboard Back, fling-dismiss is gesture Back, and a tap on an open overlay's own phone top-bar control is Back too, never a bare close that leaves the surface on the trail. A phone sheet MOUNTS only where its shell owns the lane (`sheetsEnabled`, `components/mobile/MobileMapShell.tsx`), because a mounted sheet still claims Escape on `window` and still captures focus, and CSS reaches neither. The sheet also OWNS ITS OWN STYLESHEET: `components/mobile/MobileSharedSheet.tsx` imports `components/mobile/mobileMapShell.css` itself, because PubMap renders the phone sheet through a static import while the shell that used to be the stylesheet's only importer is loaded dynamically, and on a `/map?sel=` arrival at 390 the venue sheet stood unstyled (`position: static`, `z-index: auto`) for the one to four seconds before the shell chunk landed (#1490). While `mapSelectionFrame` still waits on the slim index for a deep-linked `sel=`, `detailOpen` stays true and `PubMap` mounts `VenueSheetSkeleton` until the venue resolves. Back must restore exact held state, never reload a default, and never push history from inside a setState updater. A panel anchored to a visible trigger is not in the trail and owes only `lib/useDismissOnEscape.ts` plus outside dismiss; ambient banners and chips owe neither. Pins: `__tests__/mapSurfaceHistory.test.ts`, `__tests__/surfaceStack.test.ts`, `__tests__/mapShellSheetOwnership.test.ts`, `__tests__/pubMap.test.ts` (`mapSelectionFrame`), `e2e/map-surface-history.spec.ts`, `e2e/surface-back-and-home.spec.ts`.

<a id="a-map-control-beside-the-venue-drawer-is-an-exempt-surface-never-a-hole-in-the-t"></a>

- **A MAP CONTROL BESIDE THE VENUE DRAWER IS AN EXEMPT SURFACE, NEVER A HOLE IN THE TRAP.** Captain 13 Sep 2026 (lane 5): the desktop drawer's `map-surface` focus trap inerted the whole map stage section, so the mapped-route chip's `Check last train at final stop` took no click or Enter, and the chip sat under the drawer's left edge at 1440. A control that must stay live while the drawer is open carries `data-focus-trap-exempt` (`FOCUS_TRAP_EXEMPT_ATTRIBUTE`, `lib/useFocusTrap.ts`). FOUR rules. (1) Inert is inherited, so the trap walks DOWN a sibling that holds an exempt surface and inerts only the children beside its path (`inertTargets`), re-derived while the trap holds, because adding a second stop from the drawer mounts the chip. (2) Tab from the drawer's last control reaches the exempt surface and returns (`nextTrapFocus`), and the drawer drops `aria-modal` while the chip is live; when an exempt surface leaves with focus inside it (the chip's `Hide`), focus returns to the drawer's first control unless a strict modal is active; a `strict-modal` trap exempts nothing. Shared ownership and suspension follow the contract in `lib/useFocusTrap.ts`. The phone primary navigation also carries the exemption. Half and full shared sheets omit `aria-modal`, and Tab includes only exempt controls without inert or aria-hidden ancestors. Strict modal traps make the navigation inert. User-facing controls are described in [README.md](../../README.md#features). (3) An exempt chip takes the map lane the drawer leaves from 769px, where the drawer is a side panel, at its full 44px buttons; from 641px to 768px the drawer is a full-width bottom sheet with no lane, so the chip leaves the layout while it is open (`components/map/mapBannerStaging.css`). (4) The desktop toolbar is exempt too, from 1024px (QA journeys F04): it was inert and drawn at full strength, so Search, Filters and the drink button took no click beside an open pub, and the drawer drops `aria-modal` for it. Below 1024px the trap still holds it. The drink tray closes on the edge where a pub drawer, the planner or a landmark story opens, because it sat over the mapped route; a reader may reopen it beside the drawer. Pins: `__tests__/focusTrapExemptSurface.test.ts`, `__tests__/mapToolbarDrawerLane.test.tsx`, `e2e/drawer-route-chip-reachable.spec.ts`, `e2e/map-toolbar-beside-drawer.spec.ts`.

- **A SHEET SAYS HOW MUCH OF ITSELF IS OFF SCREEN, ONCE, AND THE SHELL CANNOT BE SCROLLED.** UI sweep 5 Sep 2026 (#1516). At 641-768 the inline map drawer is a viewport-tall bottom sheet translated down by its snap, so 45dvh of its scrollport hangs under the screen at `half`. The venue command bar restated that translate as a dvh inset, and the map shell (class `appShell`) was `overflow: hidden`, which is still a scroll container: one `scrollIntoView` on a price row scrolled the shell 403px, the drawer's box moved, the inset did not, and the bar sat mid-viewport over "Published price". THREE rules. (1) The shell is `overflow: clip`, so nothing can scroll it. (2) `SpringDrawer` publishes the px it translates by as `--drawer-sheet-offset`, and the 768 band in `app/globals.css` derives `--sheet-hidden-below` from it ONCE (the per-snap dvh declarations beside the transforms are the fallback for the frame before the spring's first) and ends the scroll body with an `::after` spacer of that height so the last row is reachable. A spacer, never padding, because Chromium measures a sticky inset from the scroller's CONTENT edge. (3) A bottom-pinned child reads that variable for its inset, and the scroll container declares its viewing region with `scroll-padding-bottom` (the hidden part plus the bar), because `scrollIntoView` and a focus scroll aim at the scrollport and would otherwise land a row under the bar. <a id="the-map-arrives-with-one-slight-turn-and-then-holds-still"></a>

- **THE MAP ARRIVES WITH ONE SLIGHT TURN, AND THEN HOLDS STILL.** Captain 7 Sep 2026: "I also want the map to rotate slightly." FOUR cases make no move at all - reduced motion, a deep link that named a pub or a landmark, a bearing somebody already owns, and a bearing that is not a number. It is NOT the idle orbit returning: `arrivalBearingSpentRef` spends it once, because `mapInstanceReady` is set once on first mount and never changes, preventing the arrival bearing effect from restarting across WebGL context rebuilds. It rides the one camera lane as a REACTIVE intent, so a reader holding the map keeps their own attitude (`lib/mapGestureGuard.ts`). TWO further facts, both measured on 7 Sep 2026. (1) THE TURN WAITS FOR THE MAP TO STOP, and the window lives in the rule leaf because the browser spec waits out the same window: without the wait the opening-location answer cancelled the turn's pending frame on the latest-wins camera lane, and with the spec keeping its own shorter copy it read the bearing DURING the wait and reported a flat map on a build that turns in 3.9 seconds. (2) THE MAP ARRIVES AT ONE OF TWO ATTITUDES and the app does not pick which: flat 0 when the canvas is built while the opening-location question is open, or the city's own designed bearing (London -8) when that question answers first. A flat map turns and an attitude is kept, so the map is off north either way; a browser spec may assert THAT, and only the unit test may assert the four.
