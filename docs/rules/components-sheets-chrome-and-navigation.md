<a id="no-surface-is-a-dead-end-and-the-way-out-is-one-pattern"></a>

- **No surface is a dead end, and the way out is one pattern.** `lib/surfaceStack.ts` holds the presentation-free trail. On the Map, `components/map/pubmap/useMapSurfaceNavigation.ts` is the only browser-history owner: every entry carries the complete trail plus venue selection state, direct opens publish through `commitOpen` before React changes drawers, and opens that land before the owner's first layout init queue on `pendingOpensRef` and flush in that same init pass so a deep-linked `sel=` is not dropped while history restores, and its one `popstate` listener restores the landed snapshot. An internal open may only `pushState` or `replaceState`; reopening a surface already lower in the trail writes a fresh entry with the current surface as its parent and never calls `history.go()`. `components/ui/surface-nav.tsx` is the only affordance: leading Back, trailing Home, the same box in the same two cells of one head, on the phone sheet, desktop drawers and List view. Back renders only when there is a parent; Escape is keyboard Back, fling-dismiss is gesture Back, and a tap on an open overlay's own phone top-bar control is Back too, never a bare close that leaves the surface on the trail. A phone sheet MOUNTS only where its shell owns the lane (`sheetsEnabled`, `components/mobile/MobileMapShell.tsx`), because a mounted sheet still claims Escape on `window` and still captures focus, and CSS reaches neither. The sheet also OWNS ITS OWN STYLESHEET: `components/mobile/MobileSharedSheet.tsx` imports `components/mobile/mobileMapShell.css` itself, because PubMap renders the phone sheet through a static import while the shell that used to be the stylesheet's only importer is loaded dynamically, and on a `/map?sel=` arrival at 390 the venue sheet stood unstyled (`position: static`, `z-index: auto`) for the one to four seconds before the shell chunk landed (#1490). While `mapSelectionFrame` still waits on the slim index for a deep-linked `sel=`, `detailOpen` stays true and `PubMap` mounts `VenueSheetSkeleton` until the venue resolves. Back must restore exact held state, never reload a default, and never push history from inside a setState updater. Explicit Close and Home release logical sheet ownership and focus immediately. An exiting presentation is inert, hidden from accessibility tools and unable to intercept pointer input. Its spring cannot dismiss a later surface. Navigation or an account change removes the retained presentation. Pins: `__tests__/mobileSharedSheetRevealSettle.test.tsx`. A panel anchored to a visible trigger is not in the trail and owes only `lib/useDismissOnEscape.ts` plus outside dismiss; ambient banners and chips owe neither. Pins: `__tests__/mapSurfaceHistory.test.ts`, `__tests__/surfaceStack.test.ts`, `__tests__/mapShellSheetOwnership.test.ts`, `__tests__/pubMap.test.ts` (`mapSelectionFrame`), `e2e/map-surface-history.spec.ts`, `e2e/surface-back-and-home.spec.ts`.

<a id="a-map-control-beside-the-venue-drawer-is-an-exempt-surface-never-a-hole-in-the-t"></a>

- **A MAP CONTROL BESIDE THE VENUE DRAWER IS AN EXEMPT SURFACE, NEVER A HOLE IN THE TRAP.** Captain 13 Sep 2026 (lane 5): the desktop drawer's `map-surface` focus trap inerted the whole map stage section, so the mapped-route chip's `Check last train at final stop` took no click or Enter, and the chip sat under the drawer's left edge at 1440. A control that must stay live while the drawer is open carries `data-focus-trap-exempt` (`FOCUS_TRAP_EXEMPT_ATTRIBUTE`, `lib/useFocusTrap.ts`). FOUR rules. (1) Inert is inherited, so the trap walks DOWN a sibling that holds an exempt surface and inerts only the children beside its path (`inertTargets`), re-derived while the trap holds, because adding a second stop from the drawer mounts the chip. (2) Tab from the drawer's last control reaches the exempt surface and returns (`nextTrapFocus`), and the drawer drops `aria-modal` while the chip is live; when an exempt surface leaves with focus inside it (the chip's `Hide`), focus returns to the drawer's first control unless a strict modal is active; a `strict-modal` trap exempts nothing. Shared ownership and suspension follow the contract in `lib/useFocusTrap.ts`. The phone primary navigation also carries the exemption. Half and full shared sheets omit `aria-modal`, and Tab includes only exempt controls without inert or aria-hidden ancestors. Strict modal traps make the navigation inert. User-facing controls are described in [README.md](../../README.md#features). (3) An exempt chip takes the map lane the drawer leaves from 769px, where the drawer is a side panel, at its full 44px buttons; from 641px to 768px the drawer is a full-width bottom sheet with no lane, so the chip leaves the layout while it is open (`components/map/mapBannerStaging.css`). (4) The desktop toolbar is exempt too, from 1024px (QA journeys F04): it was inert and drawn at full strength, so Search, Filters and the drink button took no click beside an open pub, and the drawer drops `aria-modal` for it. Below 1024px the trap still holds it. The drink tray closes on the edge where a pub drawer, the planner or a landmark story opens, because it sat over the mapped route; a reader may reopen it beside the drawer. Pins: `__tests__/focusTrapExemptSurface.test.ts`, `__tests__/mapToolbarDrawerLane.test.tsx`, `e2e/drawer-route-chip-reachable.spec.ts`, `e2e/map-toolbar-beside-drawer.spec.ts`.
