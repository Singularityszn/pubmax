# Prices, trust and drinks

Moved from [`lib/AGENTS.md`](../../lib/AGENTS.md). Authoritative policy detail lives here; the area index stays short.

<a id="what-a-community-price-is-allowed-to-move-is-a-policy-not-an-implementation-deta"></a>

- **What a community price is allowed to move is a policy, not an implementation detail.** A current submission is signed-in, handle-attributed and bound to the account's stable profile actor; only legacy rows stay anonymous. Every submission shows on the pub's own sheet, dated, and reaches pins, list rows and cheapest buckets only once a second independent submitter corroborates it inside the max-age window. `lib/communityPrice.ts` owns the constants and predicates, the corroboration count is derived on the store's read path (never stored, never client-supplied), and the gate is `mergeCommunityPriceSignals` in `components/map/communityPriceSignals.ts`. VISIBILITY is separate and ungated from AUTHORITY: `provisionalCommunityPriceVenueIds` (same file, second seam, a set of ids and never a `VenueSignal`) marks a first in-window report while colour, rows and buckets wait. A UK base pin wears the SAME mark through its own seam (`/api/price-submit?scope=provisional-base`, flagged in memory by `ukBasePubsToGeoJSON`; `public/data/uk_base/README.md`), and that mark is the ONLY thing a base pin ever earns. A surface's promise is therefore `CommunityPriceMapReach`, not a boolean, and the copy may claim the mark and never the colour; nothing about a pending report may travel through the price merge. Taking a price DOWN is the mirror policy: readers flag (`POST /api/price-submit { action: "report" }`), only a moderator hides (`app/api/admin/community-prices`), reporting never auto-hides, hiding never deletes. Report identity is server-derived (`lib/pintDropReportActor.server.ts`), only two distinct verified accounts trigger auto-hide, and the client `actor` field decides nothing. The one filter is `freshestPerCategory`, so a hidden row leaves the sheet, the count and the map candidate together. A PINT DROP REPORTS THE FIGURE THE DRINKER SENT, never a row a write RETURNED: `upsert_attributed_community_price_if_newer` is newer-wins and says nothing about whether it wrote or kept, so a drop built from its answer reports a price nobody submitted. `app/api/price-submit/route.ts` is the honest shape: it writes the drop from `submission.priceGbp`, reverts the price when the drop write fails, and refuses with `PAIRING_REPAIR_REQUIRED` when the revert fails too. Migration `0139` withdrew `public.create_one_tap_price_pair`; reviving that lane means giving the shared upsert a wrote-or-kept receipt FIRST. Pins: `__tests__/communityPriceModeration.test.ts`, `__tests__/oneTapPricePairRemoval.test.ts`, `__tests__/oneTapPricePairRemovalEffective.test.ts`.

A pub that drops out of a chain directory is not proof: The Kentish Drovers left the directory and still trades as a Wetherspoon, so its rows carry no marker. `lib/priceRowEligibility.mjs` (plain ESM with a `.d.mts` sidecar) is the ONE rule. A superseded row gives its pub the id, name, point and story, and nothing it served or charged; the detail artifact keeps every row so the sheet still resolves. <a id="a-price-wears-its-band-and-no-other-colour-red-expensive-yellow-average-green-ch"></a>

- **A PRICE WEARS ITS BAND AND NO OTHER COLOUR: red expensive, yellow average, green cheap.** Captain's law 2026-09-05 ("I already told you"), after the landing card painted a £6.50 pint yellow because the LISTED standing wore amber. `lib/priceBand.ts` is the ONE rule: terciles of the CITY's priced pubs (London today: cheap up to £5.20, average up to £6.15, expensive above, over 950 pubs), thresholds DERIVED by `npm run build:price-bands` into `public/data/price_bands/thresholds.json` and never typed, a city under `MIN_PRICE_BAND_SAMPLE` reading the dataset row. The borough was measured and rejected as the area (City of London's and Westminster's upper terciles are both £6.50, so the captain's own pint would read "average"); the numbers are in `docs/PRICE_BANDS.md`. FOUR rules. (1) ONE class family paints: `priceBandClass` gives `.priceBand-cheap|average|expensive`, defined once in `app/globals.css` beside the six `--price-band-*` tokens, setting the element's own hue, ink, surface and border, so `PriceBadge` (its `band` prop), a bare figure and the trust pill all read the band from the same three rules; the price plaque is NEUTRAL until a band paints it. (2) A STANDING HAS NO TONE: `PRICE_STANDING_TONE` and `TRUST_PILL_TONE` are deleted, `TrustPill` and the landing's standing chip carry the standing as a WORD and wear the band of their FIGURE, and `lib/pintTrust.ts`'s states are badge shapes. (3) IT IS A PINT BAND: a wine, a cocktail, a course or a bar's anchor asks nothing and stays in ink, and a band is never authority, so which figure a surface may paint at all is still that surface's own law. (4) `priceBucket` in `lib/communityPrice.ts` is the same answer under the map's older name, so the pin, the cluster donuts, the legend and the OG band counter moved together. A BAND NAMES WHOSE TERCILES IT WAS CUT FROM (review finding F-21): every city outside London ships `sampleSize: 0`, so `priceBandBasisFor` answers `all` for all of them and `priceBandNote` says "priced pubs across every city we hold" rather than implying the reader's own city; `venue-uk-…` carries no city prefix, so `priceBandAreaForVenue` answers no city there instead of labelling the whole national base layer London. Pins: `__tests__/priceBand.test.ts` (rule, shipped table, token contrast, the basis), `__tests__/priceBandSurfaces.test.ts` (every surface, and the tree swept for a colour from a standing), `e2e/price-colour-law.spec.ts`.

<a id="a-price-says-what-it-is-worth-and-a-modelled-figure-may-never-pass-as-a-publishe"></a>

- **A price says WHAT IT IS WORTH, and a modelled figure may never pass as a published one.** `lib/priceTier.ts` is the ONE decider and the whole vocabulary: `confirmed` (a drinker's check, 30 days), `listed` (the pub or its chain published it, carrying both a public source URL and an `observedAt`, 365 days), `estimate` (modelled here, never published by anybody) and `none`. `priceStandingFor` takes the strongest claim the evidence supports and never a stronger one, and the reason rides with the decision, so an expired listing is worded as one rather than as an empty pub. `confirmed` has NO PRODUCER: `PintDropReviewStatus` is `hidden | pending | reported`, nothing writes a `confirmationId`, and the type exists so surfaces are built against the whole vocabulary rather than retrofitted onto it. Building that path is the named follow-up. THREE FENCES. (1) An estimate may never speak with authority: `standingCarriesAuthority` answers false, and `__tests__/priceEstimateAuthorityFence.test.ts` walks the tree and fails any price-authority module that so much as mentions `lib/priceEstimate.ts`, the same fence `lib/priceHistory.ts` wears against the same modules. (2) An estimate may never print bare: `priceStandingFigure` is the ONE place a standing becomes a string, so `est. £X` cannot be lost on the way to a screen. (3) A basis must be answerable: `validate-data` refuses a chain basis without the pages it read, a region basis without its provenance, and any basis under `MIN_ESTIMATE_SAMPLE`. The engine models from two bases, narrower first (a permitted chain menu, else the pub's own region), reads only OSM-stated fields plus the point-in-polygon borough, and ships its basis as a few dozen rows (`npm run build:price-estimates`) rather than a per-pub artifact. Surfaces are `components/ui/trust-pill.tsx`, a PURE RENDERER that takes a decided standing and re-decides nothing, and `/how-we-estimate`; the pin carries the standing in its price TAG, never its rim, because the rim is the contrast lane by law. `lib/trustPill.ts` owns the pill's WORDS alone and no second tone vocabulary, after one shipped beside this and drifted within a day. Pins: `__tests__/priceTier.test.ts`, `__tests__/priceEstimate.test.ts`, `__tests__/pinPriceStanding.test.ts`, `__tests__/launchPrimitives.test.tsx`; model in `docs/plans/UK_PRICE_ESTIMATES.md`.

<a id="a-confirmation-is-minted-and-confirmed-now-has-a-producer"></a>

- **A CONFIRMATION is minted, and `confirmed` now has a producer.** This retires the "no producer" note in the standings entry above. `lib/pintDropConfirmation.ts` is the policy and `lib/pintDropConfirm.server.ts` is the ONE writer, behind two doors and no third: a SECOND INDEPENDENT REPORTER completes the pair after a priced drop lands, and a MODERATOR confirms one drop from the queue (`POST /api/pint-drops { action: "confirm" }`, `GET ?status=confirmed`). Independence is not redefined: the finder is built on `corroboratedPriceDrop` (`lib/venues.ts`), so a standing can never read Confirmed over a pin the map refuses to colour, and the drop lane's bucket is its `authorityKey`, which is ADR 0010's `submitterBucket` with the unattributed bucket EMPTY rather than shared. That bucket is why POSTING A PRICE NEEDS A VERIFIED ACTOR: profile ownership's `unlinked` demo lane allows a write with no signed-in caller, and a priced drop stored through it carries no authority key, so it can never corroborate at any age however many arrive. `gateHasVerifiedActor` (`lib/profileOwnership.ts`) is the ONE question the write path asks, and it applies the same trim the actor keys do so the gate and the key cannot disagree. An unpriced note or memory keeps the demo door, because every price lane in `lib/venues.ts` filters on a numeric `priceGbp` first, and a legacy unattributable row is never deleted: it lights the provisional mark through `provisionalPintDropVenueIds` and nothing more. Both drops of a pair carry the SAME `confirmationId`, because one agreement is one event. Migration `0140` + rollback stores it (`confirmation_id`, `confirmed_at`, `confirmation_basis`, `confirming_drop_id`), with an all-or-nothing CHECK, because a half-written confirmation cannot answer the citation the Index makes of it; the create path drops to the pre-0140 columns on a PostgREST 42703 rather than losing a drinker's price. Ageing out is a READING, never a write: the row keeps its date and `lib/priceTier.ts` decides. The read seam is `confirmedPriceInputFor`, carried on the venue signal as `confirmedPrice` and handed to `priceStandingFor`; no surface re-derives what confirmed means. THE PIN READS THE SAME FACT: `authoritativePriceDrop` (`lib/venues.ts`) is the ONE reading of what has earned the map, asking the minted confirmation through `confirmedPriceDrop` FIRST and only then falling back to `corroboratedPriceDrop`, and it is what `mergeVenueDrops`, `provisionalPriceDrop` and the drop lane in `components/map/usePintDrops.ts` all call. The order is the point: a browser is never shown an ANONYMOUS drop's `authorityKey`, because that key is a per-venue pseudonym the same account's public drop wears beside its real handle (#1440), so the client re-derivation alone left the pin greyer than the sheet over one pint. The re-derivation is KEPT as a fallback, never as a second authority: it dates every pre-0140 row and every pair the create path has not confirmed, it can only ever ADD a pub the confirmation lane is silent about, and it is asked second so it can never overturn one. `confirmedPriceInputFor` is a PROJECTION of `confirmedPriceDrop`, so the pub a standing calls Confirmed and the pub the map paints from a confirmation are one pub by construction. The record itself (`PintDropConfirmation`, `confirmationIsLive`) lives in the leaf `lib/pintDropConfirmationRecord.ts` so `lib/venues.ts` can read it without an import cycle; `lib/pintDropConfirmation.ts` re-exports every name and stays the module a reader reaches for. <a id="the-trust-story-reads-one-way-and-lib-pinttrust-ts-is-the-one-reading"></a>

- **THE TRUST STORY READS ONE WAY, AND `lib/pintTrust.ts` IS THE ONE READING.** Captain's cut for v0 (5 Sept 2026, Fable51Fix section 1): first public drop is logged-once, a second independent drinker is the trusted MARK and may lead the cheapest-pint story, and "No price yet" over a visible public drop may never return. `pintTrustFor` composes the five drop lanes in `lib/venues.ts` in order (`confirmedPriceDrop`, `corroboratedPriceDrop`, `disputedPintPrices`, `provisionalPriceDrop`, `agedPriceDrop`) into SIX states (`confirmed`, `corroborated`, `disputed`, `logged-once`, `aged-out`, `none`), every one a READING at `now`: an expired confirmation reads `aged-out` and nothing is written. `pintTrustSignalFields` is the ONE projection into `VenueSignal` (`components/map/usePintDrops.ts` calls no drop lane of its own), `PINT_TRUST_PIN_PAINT` says what each state paints (authority: band and figure; mark: the provisional badge; none: nothing) and `PINT_TRUST_LINE` what it prints. THREE rules. (1) COLOUR ON A PIN IS THE PRICE BAND ALONE (captain's law 5 Sept 2026: red dear, yellow average, green cheap, relative to the area; the thresholds and the shared colour module belong to the price-colour-law worker), so a trust state is a badge SHAPE: `components/map/canvas/geojson.ts` stamps `standing: "confirmed"` off the signal through `pintTrustPinStanding`, and `pubs-confirmed-badge` in `components/map/canvas/buildScene.ts` draws the provisional dot's berth HOLLOW over it. (3) THE CHIP IS THE SEAM: every drop-lane row on the Overview and the phone peek carries `data-pint-trust` and `data-venue-id` (`trustChipStateFor`), and the second-drinker confirm action mounts against that element, so the read PR and the write PR merge in either order. (4) TWO DRINKERS WITH TWO PRICES ARE TWO PRICES (captain 7 Sept 2026, over Grok's reading of the 08:37 deploy: Hatton held £4.50 and £4.70 and every surface said "Logged once, needs a second drinker" above a door offering "Still £4.70?"). `lib/pintDropAgreement.ts` is the drop lane's ONE agreement bar and it is EXACT: same drink, same measure, same pennies, where the 50p `agreesWithinTolerance` floor stays on `community_prices` alone. A write that lands in a split answers `price_disagrees`, carrying the prices and the REPORTER count (distinct authority keys plus each keyless drop), so one account logging two figures reads as one drinker. The Overview renders `provisional`, `disputed` and `aged` through ONE `DrinkerLogBlock`: three sibling branches for one claim is how the split arrived worded as a lone report. <a id="a-price-is-about-a-measure-and-a-half-is-held-out-rather-than-scaled-in"></a>

- **A PRICE IS ABOUT A MEASURE, AND A HALF IS HELD OUT RATHER THAN SCALED IN.** Contribution battle test D04: a Pint Drop of `drink: "Half of lager", priceGbp: 2.6` was accepted into the pint lane, a second drinker confirmed it as "£2.60 a pint", and that figure fed pin colour, the cheapest-pint buckets and the Pint Index at a pub whose pint is £5.50. The drink text was the only place a half could be said, the composer's own placeholder invited the word, and no read path ever asked it. `lib/drinkMeasure.ts` is the ONE owner of the closed set (`pint` | `half` | `other` with a free label), the default, the words, and the word-bounded table of measure terms a drink text may name; it is a pure leaf so a bundle needing the word "Half" pulls no venue index behind it. FIVE rules. (1) NOTHING IS EVER SCALED: a half of lager is not half the price of a pint of it, so doubling a figure would publish a price nobody paid; a non-pint row keeps its own figure, stays dated on the pub's own drop list with its measure printed beside it, and is held OUT. (2) ONE PREDICATE GUARDS EVERY LANE: `measureIsPint` reads an absent measure as `pint`, so no row written before migration 0147 changes lane, and `isPintPricedDrop` in `lib/venues.ts` is asked first by all four drop lanes, which is what carries it into `authoritativePriceDrop`, `mergeVenueDrops`, `provisionalPintDropVenueIds`, `pintTrustFor`, `confirmedPriceInputFor` and `findSecondReporterConfirmation` - so a half can neither be confirmed nor be the peer that confirms a pint. (3) THE MEASURE IS ASKED BEFORE THE FREE TEXT, and a priced drop claiming `pint` whose own words say half is refused with `MEASURE_ASK_LINE`; a passed-down memory about 40p halves is a story, not a price lane. (4) The Index counts a pre-0147 confirmed half out by name (`measure_not_pint`), and the measure survives a saved draft and rides the paired one-tap drop explicitly. (5) THE COMMUNITY PRICE LANE IS MEASURE-FREE, AND THE DOOR IS WHAT KEEPS IT SO. That lane carries no measure column, on the reasoning that its composer offers a closed category and no drink text, so its beer chip MEANS a pint. Review finding F-2 found the reasoning had outlived itself: #1517 made that composer the ONE primary price door on a pub's Overview, it never asked, and `lib/oneTapPintDrop.server.ts` stamped `measure: "pint"` on a value nobody was answered - D04 reached through the busiest lane. `components/map/composer/MeasureChips.tsx` is now the ONE measure control, mounted by the Pint Drop composer AND by `VenuePriceSubmit`'s beer lane, carrying its own stylesheet because those rules lived in spillComposer.css and the same row mounts on /near; `buildDrop` states nothing and carries the answer. A NON-PINT ANSWER WRITES NO COMMUNITY PRICE: `POST /api/price-submit` sends it down the lane that can hold it, a dated Pint Drop with its own measure, answers `price: null` and names the serving, and the composer's reach sentence stops promising the map, which is untrue of a half at any count. (6) THE MEASURE WORD MUST BE USED AS A MEASURE: `measureNamedInDrinkText` asks whether a spelled word is followed by a measure context word or ends the text, so "Other Half Green Diamond", "Half Moon IPA" and "Small Beer Lager" log as the pints they are while "Half of lager" and "1/2 lager" still refuse; a fraction glyph is always a measure. The 0147 backfill is deliberately BROADER, because it judges rows nobody could ask and errs toward holding one out, and its own comment is where that asymmetry is written down. Migration `0147` + rollback adds the column with two CHECKs; its backfill FLAGS by drink text and writes the measure column alone, so a flagged row keeps its price, its date, its author and its confirmation id, which is why `liveConfirmationFor` asks the measure like everything else in its module (F-7): an unfiltered read let a flagged half block two real pint drinkers from confirming and answered `already_confirmed` over the half's own figure. The store's additive-rollout guard for the two columns retries a PINT without them and REFUSES a half, because an absent measure reads as pint and that is D04 reintroduced by a retry. Pins: `__tests__/drinkMeasure.test.ts`, `__tests__/oneTapMeasureAsked.test.tsx` (which sweeps the tree for a measure literal outside the owner), `__tests__/pintDropConfirmation.test.ts`, `__tests__/pintDropsStore.test.ts`, `__tests__/pintDropMeasureMigrationEffective.test.ts` (real PostgreSQL 16), `e2e/one-tap-measure.spec.ts`; proof `docs/proof/half-pint-integrity/`, `docs/proof/one-tap-measure/`.

<a id="one-drinker-s-report-is-a-price-and-it-shows-in-its-own-lane-rather-than-moving-"></a>

<a id="a-published-pint-index-month-is-written-once"></a>

- **A published Pint Index month is written once.** `/pint-index` stays live; every CLOSED month is frozen by `npm run publish:pint-index-month` and served at `/pint-index/<YYYY-MM>` with its own CSV, OG card and `Dataset` JSON-LD. This is main's bounded implementation interpretation of D10, not a new captain quote. Months never pool counts, and no freshness condition applies.
