<a id="a-governed-price-landing-is-a-crawler-s-page-and-area-slug-is-held"></a>

- **A governed price landing is a crawler's page, and `/area/{slug}` publishes on its floor alone.** Three families publish out of the bundled Pint Price dataset: `/drink/{brand}`, `/area/{area}/drink/{brand}` and, since 30 Oct 2026 (captain, SEO audit #2126), the area root `/area/{area}`, which publishes on 15 priced pubs and on that floor alone (`lib/areaLanding.ts`). The [area-root contract](../specs/governed-priced-landings/PRODUCT.md#the-area-root-areaarea) owns redirects and publication limits. `lib/pricedLanding.ts` is the ONE seam both families share: the cheapest-first comparator, the publication floor TABLE, `publishablePricedRows` where `null` means the page does not exist so route, `generateStaticParams` and sitemap cannot disagree, the publisher and count sentences, the memoised `assignVenueToNightArea` and one JSON-LD builder, with `components/drinks/PricedLandingRows.tsx` as its rendering half. THREE rules. (1) The dataset is read ONLY through `lib/pintPriceLandingDataset.server.ts`, which delegates to `getPricedVenues()`; a loader of its own re-parses 6.7 MB per render, per metadata read, per OG card and per sitemap request. The `/borough` family reads it there too, after its own copy of that loader made it the slowest server render in `perf/route-budgets.json` (54 ms on CI, 117 ms on a laptop rig, against 10 ms once it shared the memo). `lib/boroughIndex.server.ts` then holds the two DERIVED tables the index prints, and `loadHistoricPubs` holds its own parse, both on the same rule: bundled build-time data, one read per process, and a read that FAILED is never cached, or one bad render leaves an empty city for the life of the process. That read has TWO answers: a page degrades to `[]` and 404s, the sitemap's `…OrThrow` wrapper fails loud, because a truncated sitemap deindexes what it dropped. (2) A landing CTA opens a PUB (`?sel=<venueId>`, plus `?brand=` alone), never `?q=<area name>`, which is a free-text venue filter an area name would narrow to nothing. It may name only a pub the map can RESOLVE, so `pricedLandingMapArrivalRow` takes the cheapest row inside the eager shard and `pricedLandingMapHref` drops `sel` entirely when there is none, over the TRI-STATE `lib/mapEagerVenueIndex.server.ts`; the ranked list never moves for a CTA, and `pricedLandingAreaMapCta` answers the href and the label together so a second predicate cannot re-decide it. (3) Publishing a brand-by-area pair is NARROWER than route readiness (the area root takes no readiness gate at all): `nightAreaPublishesPrices` keeps the gate version and completeness predicates and drops only `isNightAreaRouteReady`'s review expiry, because a dated price list is not a route and expiring it would 404 indexed URLs. The brand page lists those published pair pages so they are not sitemap-only. Pins: `__tests__/nightAreaReviewRenewal.test.ts` (the renewal alarm, 30 days ahead), `__tests__/sitemap.test.ts`. Contract: `docs/specs/governed-priced-landings/{PRODUCT,TECH}.md`.
