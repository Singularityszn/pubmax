# Venue, plan and message surfaces

Moved from [`components/AGENTS.md`](../../components/AGENTS.md). Authoritative policy detail lives here; the area index stays short.

<a id="arrival-is-the-moment-of-togetherness-never-an-admin-form"></a>

- **Arrival is the moment of togetherness, never an admin form.** An account that already owns a handle is owed NOTHING on arrival: `components/identity/AccountOnboarding.tsx` awaits the server answer, syncs `pubmax_handle` and resolves to nothing when a handle comes back. It may never answer with an owned-identity or rename surface, because `POST /api/identity/handle/claim` stores no date of birth and `GET /api/identity/onboarding` therefore reports `complete:false` for the life of every account claimed that way. Renaming lives in profile editing alone (`components/profile/PubmaxxAccountHub.tsx`), private details in `PrivateIdentityEditor`, and a failed status load never falls through to the claim form. The claim step is TWO BEATS and one action, never a required section plus an optional one. A genuine sign-in gets one warm line (`lib/arrivalWelcome.ts`, rendered by `components/auth/ArrivalWelcome.tsx` as a polite live region, never a dialog). Restoring a session on a page load gets none (`__tests__/authSessionTransition.test.ts`, `e2e/auth-callback-confirmation.spec.ts`). `/login` is TWO DOORS sharing one link machinery: `?mode=signin|signup` differ in copy, email intent and landing, and `?from=` returns a sign-in where the nav sent it from (`arrivalDestination` refuses off-site paths and refuses to land anyone back on a sign-in page). Pins: `__tests__/arrivalWelcome.test.ts`, `__tests__/accountOnboarding.test.ts`, `__tests__/loginPage.test.ts`, `e2e/arrival-journey.spec.ts`.

<a id="the-overview-has-one-price-door-per-trust-state-and-the-composer-is-folded-behin"></a>

- **THE OVERVIEW HAS ONE PRICE DOOR PER TRUST STATE, AND THE COMPOSER IS FOLDED BEHIND IT.** Captain's rule (core-loop battle test L03, 5 Sept 2026): one button system, one clear primary per screen. A pub Overview offered four to eight price actions on one sheet, because every block that touched a price grew its own invitation. `overviewPriceDoor` in `lib/pintTrust.ts` is the whole policy, a closed table over the five trust states: `logged-once` and `aged-out` get the confirm door from #1492 (`Still £4.50?`, seeding the Pint Drop composer), every other state and every non-drop lane gets the one log door (`LOG_PRICE_DOOR_LABEL`), and an anchor lane gets none. FOUR rules. (1) The door is decided ONCE in `VenuePriceSummary` (`components/map/inspector/VenueOverviewTab.tsx`) and appended to whichever lane block renders; no lane, nudge or block may carry a door of its own, and `FirstDropNudge` takes the door as children. (2) The community price composer (`VenuePriceEntryPanel`, `open` prop) mounts its form only when `overviewComposerOpen` says so: the sheet answered the door with a focus request, with the sign-in gate, or a ranked mission arrived for this pub (the mission IS the ask). The door folds away while the form is open, so the two never stand on one screen; the panel's effects still run folded, so the venue read and `price_submit_viewed` are unchanged. (3) The prices-by-drink invite folds wherever the price area owns the door (`drinkInviteOwnedByPriceArea`); under a drink lens the area is hidden and the block keeps the lane's door. (4) The sticky bar carries NO price action and NO painted primary: `Add price` is gone, and `Make it Stop 1` is a ghost because the phone peek's `Plan stop` is the one painted plan action. The door is the `priceDoor` class (`app/globals.css`), the same flat action accent every launch primary wears, never a price band. `UnverifiedPubSheet` keeps `VenueSheetPriceEntry` open by default and is untouched. Pins: `__tests__/overviewPriceDoor.test.ts` (policy per state, one rendered door, the fold, a source sweep for the retired doors), `e2e/overview-price-door.spec.ts` (390, signed in, five states); proof `docs/proof/overview-price-door/`.

<a id="the-bill-is-asked-for-in-front-of-the-price-never-inside-the-disclosure"></a>

- **THE BILL IS ASKED FOR IN FRONT OF THE PRICE, NEVER INSIDE THE DISCLOSURE.** Captain 7 Sept 2026: a new price comes with a photo of the bill. `lib/pintDropReceipt.ts` is the whole rule (when a bill is owed, what a photo may be, and every word said about it), and both price doors read it: the one-tap `VenuePriceSubmit` and the full Pint Drop composer's `SpillReceiptStep` (`components/map/composer/`). THREE rules ride with it. (1) A REQUIRED CONTROL MAY NOT LIVE BEHIND A DISCLOSURE. `Log it` is disabled without a bill, so asking for one inside "Add a photo or story" would refuse a drinker at the button for a control they were never shown. `SpillReceiptStep` renders under the price step, outside `spillExtras`, and only while the composer holds a price; a note or a photo of the pub claims nothing a reader has to check and is asked for nothing. (2) THE BILL FIRST, THEN THE PINT FOR FUN. `PINT_PHOTO_FUN_LINE` and its picker appear only once the bill is in, and neither blocks a submit. (3) THE COMPOSER SAYS BILL, THE DROP ROW SAYS RECEIPT. `RECEIPT_PHOTO_LABEL` names the thumbnail on the drop's own row (`PintDropsList`); the composer's button, its one line and its ready row all say bill, so neither surface rotates a synonym. Pins: `__tests__/pintDropReceipt.test.ts`, `e2e/price-bill-required.spec.ts` (390, the refusal and the send), `e2e/price-two-drinkers-receipt-proof.spec.ts`; proof `docs/proof/price-two-drinkers-receipt/`.

<a id="plan-opens-on-one-describe-first-question-not-the-wizard"></a>

- **`/plan` opens on one describe-first question, not the wizard.** `components/plan/PlanDescribeFirst.tsx` is the default entry surface at every width: one heading, a free-text field, and a few chips that generate a real route keyless. The five-step wizard (`components/plan/PlanIntake.tsx`) sits behind its own "Guide me instead" link and is otherwise unchanged. A returning visitor with a saved, incomplete wizard draft lands back on the wizard, and the ONE thing that overrides that is a Pub Pal handoff: `/plan?query=<ask>` (`PLAN_QUERY_PARAM` and `parsePlanHandoffQueryFromSearch`, `lib/planOccasion.ts`, written by `planPalRouteHandoffHref`) opens describe-first with that ask prefilled, because describe-first is the only surface that can show it. The two chip params keep the rule and keep their narrow reads: `occasion` takes closed ids, `describe` takes shipped chip text, and `query` alone takes arbitrary ask text, which is why it is read last. A URL ask is applied ONCE and is never dropped in silence: where describe-first cannot render it the ask lands in the concierge field and wins over a recovered line there, the one-shot ask draft is SPENT whichever prefill wins, and the address is re-read after the router commits. Pin: `__tests__/planComposerDescribeLink.test.ts`. A HELD pub already shows the full composer, so exactly ONE describe surface renders: `planComposerShowsDescribeFirst` and `planComposerShowsIntake` (`components/plan/PlanComposer.tsx`) hide describe-first and the incomplete wizard while `heldVenueId` holds Stop 1. A held acceptance also SEEDS the area, so `planIntakeHandoff` drops `area` from `skipped`, a step both skipped and answered being the server's `PLAN_INTAKE_MALFORMED` refusal. A held pub accepted with no area, sorted with no area named, takes its area from its own location only within a supported area in its accepted city (`venueNightArea` uses the containing-area geometry in `venueAreaClaim`). Any area the reader chose or described still wins. Without a containing area it retains the pub and shows one "Choose an area." prompt. Any refusal that still comes back goes through `planGenerationFailureStatus`, the ONE owner of that sentence for `#plan-route-status` and the composer's own error notice alike: one house-voice line, never the server plumbing string, and "The earlier route is still here" only when a route is already on screen. `/plan` renders `PlanComposer` alone; `MobilePlanActivation` is the map sheet's phone planner (`components/PubMap.tsx`) and never mounts on that route, so the two surfaces need separate specs.

<a id="a-describe-chip-is-a-promise-of-a-route-and-the-promise-is-made-through-the-desc"></a>

- **A describe chip is a promise of a route, and the promise is made through the DESCRIBE-FIRST body.** Every shipped chip string must return a priced three-stop route for the body the chip actually sends: `{ query, intake }` with every wizard step skipped, which takes the GROUNDED optimizer. A bare `{ query }` curl takes the legacy path and will happily pass a chip the real surface 422s on. The sharpest trap is vocabulary: `inferNightContext` turns "walk" or "tube" into a `transportConstraint`, and `selectGroundedPlanRoute` (`lib/planRouteOptimizer.ts`) refuses outright the moment a context carries one, so "river walk" 422s while "riverside" builds. A chip LABEL may still say walk, because a label never reaches the optimizer. `__tests__/cultureCrawlGenerateRoute.test.ts` sends the real body; `__tests__/cultureCrawl.test.ts` pins the empty-transportConstraints rule.

<a id="the-plan-result-is-a-route-and-the-settings-sit-behind-it"></a>

- **THE PLAN RESULT IS A ROUTE, AND THE SETTINGS SIT BEHIND IT.** Design lane 1, 7 Oct 2026. After "Sort it" the page used to be a settings form with the stops at the foot as editable text fields. Once `routeSorted` is true and a stop exists, `components/plan/PlanComposer.tsx` uses `resultMode` and `data-result` to show the route. The heading reads the saved `routeNight` area and daypart against the start time's night (`routeHeading`), a night running until 05:00 London so an early-hours start belongs to the evening before: the same night is "Tonight in Clapham" or "Today in Soho", the next is "Tomorrow night in Clapham", later ones name the weekday ("Saturday night in Clapham") and, more than six nights ahead, the date as well ("Wednesday night in Clapham, 14 Oct"), and a start already past reads "Your night in Clapham", past by the same instant the lock resolves. An unknown area reads "Your route". `routeSummaryLine` in `lib/planRouteView.ts` summarises resolved stops, price each and total walk in one mono line. It omits a total when a required figure is absent. `PlanRouteMiniMap variant="strip"` uses the existing canvas and `--route-line`. The draw runs once per ordered venue sequence in a tab through `lib/routeDraw.ts`. On a phone the strip sits above the cards. At 1024px and wider it sits beside them. Without WebGL2 or two located stops the strip is absent. A reorder redraws the same located pubs on the same map; it never tears the strip down. The route has one card per Stop (`components/plan/PlanStopList.tsx`) with the walk between them. FIVE rules. (1) A STOP IS A VENUE RECORD, NEVER A STRING: there is no "Venue name" input. A stop with no pub yet (one just added) shows a pub finder and becomes a card when a pub is picked (`pickedPlanStop`). (2) A FIGURE IS THE STOP'S OWN OR IT IS ABSENT: `DraftStop` carries the generator's `walkingMinutesFromPrevious` with the neighbour it was timed from (`walkFromVenueId`) only when that walk was routed on foot (`ROUTED_WALK_TRANSPORT_BASIS`), and the map's measured legs count only when routed too, so a straight-line estimate is never printed as a walk, `estimatedPintPricePence` and `priceKind`; a swap or a new pub clears them (`NO_TIMING_OR_PRICE`), a reorder breaks the neighbour match so the walk is re-timed from the map's own legs (`onLegs`, `measuredLegMinutes`) or left blank, and a total that needs every stop is dropped when one lacks it. The pint band colours a pint price only (`priceBandClass`); a selected non-beer drink price stays neutral and keeps its "community report" line. (3) A REORDER KEEPS A LEGACY PROOF AND BREAKS A V2 ONE: the V1 grounding proof commits to the SET of venues, so `composerRouteMutation({ reorder: true })` keeps it; an anchored route carries a V2 proof that binds the exact order (`lib/planGrounding.server.ts`), so there a reorder is an identity change and asks for a refresh. A held pub is never lifted and nothing lands above it. (4) THE REQUEST CONTROLS ARE BEHIND "Tune details". `PlanTuneSheet` opens at its half detent on a phone. Above 640px it is a centred dialog. The sheet holds the description box, "Sort it again", the editable context and the templates. The night name, creator name, start time and Area coverage remain below the cards. The stale-route notice and its Regenerate button stay on the page. A new route closes the sheet. On a phone, "Lock it in" stays above the tab bar. `PlanResultProvenance` dates the pub list through `PUB_LIST_REFRESHED_CAPTION`, not any individual price. (5) MOTION AND TOUCH FOLLOW THE SKILLS: the card drag is 1:1 from the grab point (`components/plan/useStopGestures.ts`), a touch lifts after a still 350ms hold so a scroll that starts on a card is still a scroll, the drop settles on a spring (response 0.3, damping 0.8: the release carried momentum), a swipe left or keyboard focus shows Remove, a mouse gets Remove as a plain button beside the card once the list is 520px wide (a container query) and, narrower, a More button slides the card over to show it, Alt and the arrow keys move a card, Delete or Backspace removes it, reduced motion lands every move at once. A changed order, through a drop or the keyboard, uses `selection-kept`. A remove uses `selection-released`, and creation uses `plan-locked`. `lib/nativeHaptics.ts` owns the engine table and native-only, reduced-motion gates. A sheet detent or a slot crossing ticks nothing. Pins: `__tests__/planRouteView.test.ts`, `__tests__/planStopReorder.test.ts`, `__tests__/routeDraw.test.ts`, `__tests__/planComposerRouteView.test.ts`, `__tests__/planStopList.test.tsx`, `__tests__/planTuneSheet.test.tsx`, `__tests__/planRouteMiniMapRace.test.ts`, `e2e/plan-route-result.spec.ts`, `e2e/plan-route-strip.spec.ts`.

<a id="a-plan-read-follows-the-capability-and-the-invite-token-is-one-live-value"></a>

- **A PLAN READ FOLLOWS THE CAPABILITY, AND THE INVITE TOKEN IS ONE LIVE VALUE.** Core-loop battle test, 5 Sep 2026. Every plan surface server-renders the privacy-safe preview and upgrades itself through the capability-gated `GET /api/plans/[id]`, and that read used to run ONCE, at mount: a host on a second device read before the seat claim landed and kept the invitee teaser on their own plan (M01), and a guest who tapped "I'm in" got their crew row while the route beside it still said it reveals once you join (M02). `components/plan/usePlanMemberRead.ts` is the rule: read at mount, and read again each time the capability is not the one the LAST read carried. That last-read token is what keeps a member to one read, because a naive dependency on the token re-runs the moment a mount-time restore publishes its own answer. Restoring the session is NOT the hook's job: it belongs to the surfaces that own a session lane and gate it on `identityResolved`, since asking earlier cannot spend the recovery PATCH (`__tests__/activePlanAccountClaim.test.tsx` holds that gate). A read that comes back as the PREVIEW is an ANSWER, so it also takes the route back down when a capability is revoked, on EVERY surface: `isPlanPreviewProjection` lives in `lib/planPrivacy.ts`, the module that owns the projection's `visibility` discriminant, because a second copy of it is how `components/night/NightModeCard.tsx` kept showing a revoked member the route and the get-in report (F-33). The REQUEST is one per Plan: `readPlanMemberProjection` (same module as the hook) is the shared `inFlight` promise the route, the crew and the Night Mode card all spend, and `__tests__/surfaceReadFence.test.ts` carries the one row for it (F-34). What `PlanSummaryMember` does with a later read is the other half: it seeds from props once, so it tracks the revision it last ADOPTED FROM and adopts anything else DURING RENDER, never in an effect, because a revision is a string or a number and cannot be compared for freshness, and a save that moved `savedRevision` past the prop may not be reverted by it (F-30). Beside it, `lib/planInviteTokenClient.ts` is the ONE live invite token per plan in the `lib/planSessionCapability.ts` idiom, because a rotate used to leave the Send on WhatsApp href carrying the retired token the server already refuses (M04); its answer is FOUR-WAY, concurrent surfaces share one read, and `unavailable` is RE-ASKED like `unknown`, because a read we could not run is a fact about us rather than an answer about this Plan and one blip used to leave the host a bare `/plan/{id}` link for the life of the page (F-31). Two smaller laws ride here: the host name a composer offers is the PUBLIC HANDLE and never the email local part, which was going out as the public host name on the plan, the share card and the unfurler (`creatorNameFromAccount` in `lib/crew.ts`, L05); and the route rail in `app/plan/plan.css` is the ROUTE's spine, so the teaser does not draw one, having no route. Pins: `__tests__/planInviteLiveToken.test.ts`, `__tests__/crewCreatorName.test.ts`, `e2e/plan-join-reveal.spec.ts`, `e2e/plan-invite-surface.spec.ts`, `e2e/plan-host-name.spec.ts`; proof `docs/proof/plan-join-reveal/`.

<a id="a-message-bubble-s-width-is-the-row-s-business-and-a-message-photo-is-the-one-ow"></a>

- **A message bubble's WIDTH is the row's business, and a message photo is the one owned image that is not public.** THE BUBBLE FIRST, a rule and not a styling choice: `.messageLine` (`app/messages/messages.css`) carries the max-width plus `width: fit-content`, and `.messageBubble` may name no percentage but 100%. The limit on the bubble clamped it to a fraction of its OWN natural width and `overflow-wrap: anywhere` then broke mid-word to obey; `fit-content` is the other half, flooring on the AVAILABLE width where a flex item's automatic minimum floors on min-content, which under `anywhere` is one character. THE ATTACHMENT SECOND: `lib/messageAttachments.ts` is the pure closed set (`photo` | `venue`), the storage keys and every word these surfaces print. A photo takes the ENTIRE owned-image journey (`lib/messagePhotoMedia.server.ts` over `lib/uploadedImage.server.ts`), and its bytes are NOT public: `lib/messagePhotoServe.server.ts` re-applies `requireLinkedActor` plus the participant pair, answers `private, no-store`, and the thread reads it through `authedFetch` into an object URL (`components/messages/MessagePhoto.tsx`), because an `<img src>` cannot carry a bearer. REPORTING is the takedown: a flagged message loses its attachment in the ONE projection both store backends read (`rowToAttachment` in `lib/messagesStore.ts`), so the thread, the serve route and the corroborating read stop together. A SHARED PUB stores a venue id and nothing else, so no coordinate is written and the viewer-coordinate egress law is untouched, and `lib/messageAttachmentCards.server.ts` resolves name, area and a figure on every read, because a price frozen into a message is an undated claim nobody can correct. The curated matcher behind it is shared with Wanted (`lib/curatedVenueSearch.server.ts`, route `/api/venue-search`). Backends are selected by the ID's whole shape through `isMemoryConversationId` (`lib/messagesStore.ts`), and every store-selection sentinel must use that predicate. Opening a conversation is a WRITE like sending one: ONE limiter sits above the action switch, and the recipient handle must resolve to a live profile before any row is written. Migration `0102` + rollback, whose tombstone takes a leaving account's message PHOTOS while leaving the conversation's words. What the recipient DOWNLOADS is the stored JPEG, so the box is `MESSAGE_PHOTO_MAX_EDGE` at `MESSAGE_PHOTO_JPEG_QUALITY` through `prepareUploadedImage`'s `jpegQuality` option, which every other slot leaves at its default. The crop step is a MODAL and has to be one: it sits above the phone tab bar and reserves that bar's lane, traps focus, takes focus on open and leaves on Escape. The full-frame viewer is bounded on a phone and a backdrop tap closes it, so one photograph never reads as a page. Pins: `__tests__/messagesStoreIdRouting.test.ts`, `__tests__/messagesRoute.test.ts`, `__tests__/messageBubbleAndComposer.test.ts`, `__tests__/messageAttachments.test.ts`, `__tests__/messageAttachmentsRoute.test.ts`, `__tests__/messagePhotoQuality.test.ts`, `__tests__/messageCropOverlayRender.test.ts`, `e2e/message-bubble-geometry.spec.ts`.

<a id="a-thread-reads-like-a-conversation-and-its-composer-is-pinned-over-the-foot-of-t"></a>

- **A THREAD READS LIKE A CONVERSATION, AND ITS COMPOSER IS PINNED OVER THE FOOT OF THE PAGE.** Captain 1 October 2026 chose Instagram as the messaging reference: flat neutral panes, compact headers, outgoing bubbles in the launch accent (`--brass` with `--color-on-accent` ink, as on every other primary) and a recipient picker shared by direct messages and groups. The picker searches the existing public profile directory and creates a conversation only from explicitly selected people. It keeps no persistent recipient cache; closing it or changing account cancels pending reads and prevents late navigation. The strict modal restores focus on close and uses `lib/keyboardInset.ts` at every viewport width to keep its actions above the keyboard. This visual exception belongs to messages and does not retune the rest of the app. The September layout measurements at 390, 768 and 1440, light and dark, are historical evidence in `docs/proof/messaging-ui/`; they predate the recipient picker and the launch-accent bubbles. FOUR rules. (1) `lib/messageTimeline.ts` is the ONE reading of a thread and it is a pure leaf: a day line between London days (Today, Yesterday, the weekday, then the date), a RUN of consecutive bubbles from one person inside `MESSAGE_RUN_GAP_MS` that share a straight shoulder, the time folded under every bubble until it is tapped, hovered or reached by keyboard (`data-revealed`, `:focus-within`), and a read state on the viewer's OWN newest message alone that says only what the row proves, Seen or Sent, never Delivered. The inbox row's compact time is the same module's `inboxTimeLabel`. (2) THE COMPOSER IS PINNED, and the phone has two things under it: the tab bar's lane, which `lib/softKeyboard.ts` says has stepped aside when a keyboard is up, and the keyboard itself, whose height `lib/keyboardInset.ts` measures off `visualViewport` because no phone browser shrinks the layout viewport for one. Both ride the thread as data on the `messageThreadShell` element (`display: contents`) and the stylesheet decides the geometry; a thread page and `/pal/chat` carry `pageHidesCreateFab` because the floating compose control sat over Send. On a phone the PAGE is the scroller and `scrollToNewest` scrolls to its foot, because a sticky dock covers whatever `scrollIntoView` aligns; in the desktop split (`min-width: 1024px`) the list scrolls on its own inside a fixed-height panel. (3) A SEND LANDS AT ONCE: an outbox bubble and a cleared field before the server answers, and a refused send takes the bubble back and puts the words back in the field. (4) A face is `components/messages/MessageAvatar.tsx`: the person's photo when the row carries `otherAvatarUrl`, otherwise a first letter drawn from whatever is PRINTED beside it (`label`, the display name when there is one and the handle when there is not). The inbox read joins `otherDisplayName` and `otherAvatarUrl` onto each DIRECT row in one batch read of the public cards (`lib/messageParticipantCards.server.ts`), a thread header wears the same card through `usePublicProfileCard`, and a group row is never lent a person's face or name. A name is printed with the handle under or beside it (`conversationRowHandle`, `threadHeaderSecondaryLine`), never the handle twice. Opening a thread marks what was waiting as read, and the thread announces it (`lib/messagesUnreadSignal.ts`) so the nav badge and the open thread's inbox row drop at once instead of at their next read (`__tests__/messagesLinkUnread.test.tsx`, `__tests__/messageThreadUnreadSignal.test.tsx`, `__tests__/messagesInboxReadSignal.test.tsx`). A keyless server cannot verify a bearer, so the browser fences answer `/api/messages*` in the page (`e2e/messages-thread.spec.ts`) and measure rendered boxes: composer in view with a keyboard-sized viewport, newest message above the dock after a send, Send a 44px circle with the arrow centred to the pixel, no sideways scroll at 320. Pins: `__tests__/messageTimeline.test.ts`, `__tests__/keyboardInset.test.ts`, `__tests__/messageBubbleAndComposer.test.ts`, `__tests__/messagesRecipientPicker.test.tsx`, `e2e/messages-recipient-picker.spec.ts`, `e2e/messages-thread.spec.ts`, `e2e/pal-chat-composer.spec.ts`.
