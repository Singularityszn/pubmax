# Account deletion, legal row and the You tab's sign-in door

Proof for the three store-blocking gaps closed together, because all three land
on the same surface: the You tab (`/u/you`). Every shot is 390x844, the phone
width a store reviewer holds.

## What each gap was

| Gap | The defect |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | No in-app account deletion existed anywhere. App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(v) and Play's 2024 policy both require it for an app that offers account creation, and `docs/STORE_READINESS.md` already promised both stores it worked in the submitted build. |
| 3 | No privacy notice, terms or support contact was reachable from inside the app shell. The shell starts on `/tonight` (`SHELL_START_PATH`, `lib/entryDecision.ts`) and the phone nav has no More menu, so a native user could go weeks without opening `/`, which is the only page carrying those links. |
| 4 | Signed out, the You tab said "Sign in" three times and contained no link to `/login` at all. Its own primary action, "Claim your @handle", was an in-page anchor to `#account-settings`, which is the block that restates the same invitation, so the one call to action scrolled the reader to a restatement of the problem. |

## The shots

| File | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| `before-you-signed-out-390.png` | The You tab signed out, before. Three sign-in sentences, nothing to tap, no legal links. Taken during the mobile gap audit (4 September 2026) against a production build at `67c9b37dd`. |

| `after-delete-confirm-390.png` | The deletion confirm step, signed in. Both lists before anything is sent: what leaves, what stays. |

The two `after-` shots are produced by `e2e/account-store-doors.spec.ts`, so they
are regenerated by the suite rather than taken by hand and left to rot:

```
npx playwright test e2e/account-store-doors.spec.ts --project=chromium
```

The signed-in shot uses the existing auth doubles (`e2e/helpers/authDoubles.ts`),
so it needs no real account and spends no real session.

## What the confirm step promises, and why

The words are `lib/accountDeletion.ts`, shared by the in-app confirm and the
public `/account/delete` page, so the two cannot promise different things. Every
line is a statement the database already makes true: migration `0078`'s BEFORE
DELETE trigger on `auth.users`, extended by `0096`, `0097`, `0098` and `0102`,
stamps the profile tombstone, drops the owned images and their bytes, clears the
private card fields and takes the photos out of message threads. The handle stays
reserved and logged prices stay attributed because the profile ROW survives as a
tombstone, which is the designed behaviour rather than a limitation.
