## 1. Map List's Cheapest control ignores serving comparability

Reachable source path: `PubMap.tsx:1909` builds `drinkLensPrices` with `discoveryDrinkLensPrices`; the list model at line 3021 receives the active lens. `MapVenueList.tsx:152-158` offers **Cheapest** for that model. `lib/mapVenueList.ts:155-164` extracts only the positive finite `priceGbp` from the active lens, and lines 251-264 sort those amounts ascending. The row at lines 213-218 displays category and amount, dropping the available serving, source and observation date. Neither operation consults `listedServingComparisonKey` or otherwise groups compatible servings.

The inputs can genuinely contain mixed known/unknown servings. `GET /api/price-submit?drinkCategory=gin` at `app/api/price-submit/route.ts:560-574` combines community and listed indices. `lib/ukPriceBundle.server.ts:90-100` selects one listed quote per venue from `listedCategoryPrices`. That producer groups equal recognised ml servings within a venue and preserves unranked unknown quotes (`listedCategoryPrices.ts:103-147`), but returns both; its cross-venue index does not establish a shared serving group. `readListedDrinkIndex` preserves `servingSize` (`mapExperienceLens.ts:411-424`), so the later List comparator loses a distinction that was available.

Community trust and serving comparability are separate. `trustedDrinkLensPrices` at `mapExperienceLens.ts:80-92` requires the selected category and `drivesMap(mapCandidateOf(row), now)`; `communityPrice.ts:391-396` requires corroboration and age. A lone uncorroborated report does not reach this ranking through that path. Even an admitted community category quote becomes a MapLensPrice with no serving field. `discoveryDrinkLensPrices:433-438` prefers that trusted community quote over a listed quote for the same venue, including a listed quote whose serving is known. Valid listed source/date/standing is also required. Estimates are not introduced into this non-beer lens, and the active lens's missing price does not fall back to a cheaper beer baseline (`mapVenueListSortPrice`).

Thus the source establishes a real inconsistent ranking contract: a lower amount for an unknown gin/wine serving can precede a higher amount for a known serving under the visible Cheapest control. It does not establish that the lower quote is invalid, currently sold, or better value. Existing `__tests__/mapVenueList.test.ts:224` asserts amount ordering for an active soft-drink lens without serving metadata; it does not protect a known/unknown serving pair. Future correction belongs to the Map owner after native reproduction, not a generic quarantine or a new price authority.

## 2. Area/place results and Plan value selection already avoid that amount comparison

The Map area/locality sheet receives the same lens prices. `lib/areaButton.ts:347-367` price-sorts only the default pint lane; selected-drink rows with a quote precede unpriced rows, then order by distance/name. Its row at lines 373-385 includes serving or explicit unknown and listed source/date. `AreaSheet.tsx:216-221` calls the non-beer result **Wine/Gin prices**, reserving **Cheapest pints** for the default lane. It uses neutral discovery even when two listed servings happen to match, rather than falsely ranking their unlike amounts.

The `/places` route is the city picker, not a wine/spirits venue-ranking surface. `app/places/PlacesClient.tsx:340-350` shows city-level listed-pint coverage; this does not rank wine or gin or assert that either has the cheapest offer. A searched place on the Map uses the area/locality path above.

For generated Plans, `planGenerationRanking.ts:20-27` gives a fixed four-point fit boost for attributable evidence in the requested category, independently of price amount/serving. Its value branch at lines 40-51 gives a fixed one-point boost for a corroborated selected-category community quote; it uses declining beer-price scores only when the requested category is absent/beer. Known listed and unknown listed quotes receive the same evidence-fit boost, not a lower-pence advantage. `planGeneration.server.ts:220-234` preserves the selected listed quote's category, serving, source and date; `planGenerationDto.ts:79-80,83-105` disables pint pricing for non-beer and preserves listed evidence or explicit unknown-serving community evidence. `planSelectedDrinkPriceEvidence.ts:69-75` describes the exact category/source/date and serving or absence.

No cheaper-beer substitution or mixed-serving lowest-price scoring was found in this bounded Plan path. Presence/authority boosts do not prove a cheapest Plan and should not be represented as that claim. This review did not audit every optional concierge/social/value feature.
