# Product copy review R20

Source-only review of Map, Places, Plan, Create, login and onboarding copy. No repository edits or runtime checks. Three actionable findings.

1. **Map price cap overpromises what stays on the map.** `lib/mapPriceLegend.ts:573` says: “Show pubs at or under this pint price.” The control sets a maximum pint price, but `filterVenues` deliberately keeps null-price pubs and every non-pub venue (`lib/venues.ts:1220-1226`); `__tests__/venues.test.ts:217-221` pins the null-price behavior. Readers can set a cap and still see unpriced pubs. Suggested copy: “Shows priced pubs at or under this cap. Unpriced pubs and other venues stay.” The mismatch is proven by source and an existing behavior test. Check rendered fit in the map key on a narrow viewport before adopting the longer line.

2. **Places promises future price coverage without a schedule.** `lib/places.ts:76-78, 110-115` shows “Prices coming” for every city whose price capability is not `available`. `lib/cityCapabilities.ts:35-39, 77-90` describes those cities as not yet collected, with no collection date or commitment. The row pill turns “not available” into a future promise. Suggested replacement: “No listed pint prices yet.” This names the price lane and avoids claiming there are no community reports. `__tests__/placesTab.test.ts:178-204` currently codifies the promise and should follow any copy correction. This is a source-level mismatch; check pill fit in the city list at 320 px before adoption.

3. **Signup gives an unsupported one-minute estimate.** `components/auth/LoginPage.tsx:74-80` promises: “Takes a handle and a minute.” The route offers email and social sign-in, then the first-account flow asks for a handle after authentication (`components/identity/AccountOnboardingFrame.tsx:24-30`). The minute estimate is neither bounded by the flow nor measured. Keep the useful benefit sentence and replace only the estimate: “Log a pint price, plan a crawl, keep your nights. Choose a sign-in, then pick a handle.” The wording and path are source-confirmed; no browser timing or real-provider run was performed.

Plan and Create copy reviewed without a stronger source-level finding. Onboarding's “checked routes” is left unflagged: it selects areas whose `gate.passed` is true, and that gate is explicitly the route-ready gate. This review does not establish how any revised line fits or reads in a live browser.
