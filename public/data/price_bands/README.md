# Price band thresholds

`thresholds.json` is the price band table `lib/priceBand.ts` reads. It is
generated by `npm run build:price-bands` from the enabled city packs in
`lib/cityVenuePacks.mjs` and must not be edited by hand.

- `rule`: `terciles`. The cheapest third of a city's priced pubs is cheap,
  the middle third average, the dearest third expensive.
- `minSample`: the fewest priced pubs a city needs for its own row.
- `all`: the thresholds over every priced pub in every pack.
- `cities`: one row per city with at least `minSample` priced pubs.
- `sampleSizes`: the priced pub count of every enabled city, including the
  ones with no row.

`__tests__/priceBand.test.ts` recomputes this file from the packs and fails
when it drifts. The rule and the London numbers are in `docs/PRICE_BANDS.md`.
